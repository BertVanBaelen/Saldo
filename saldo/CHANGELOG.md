# Changelog

## 0.9.0
- Notifications (version 0.9, plan in plan/v0.9-notifications): Saldo now tells you by **push on your phone** and by **e-mail** when an account heads into its buffer or below zero, when a bank link expires or stops, when someone joins or invites you, when a contribution is missing, when bank rows wait for a category and when a new device signs in, and, if you switch them on, sends a **week and a month overview** as a receipt. You choose per type and per channel on the new Meldingen screen. **Take a Home Assistant backup before updating**: the update runs two migrations (0018: push subscriptions and the push key, which rebuilds the push table; 0019: the channel settings and the notification log, which rebuilds the log), and the add-on also keeps a database copy in `/data/backups` (the last five are kept) before it migrates. **The first start after the update sends nothing**: every account that is already red or in the buffer, every reminder already sent, every open join or invitation and every missing contribution is recorded silently, so only changes after the update notify. Rolling back means restoring the backup.
- Push notifications (Android and iPhone): Meldingen has a switch "Pushmeldingen" ("Op dit toestel") and a button "Stuur een testmelding". Saldo sends the pushes itself, with its own sender and no extra account or service, to the installed app (the push service of Google or Apple only relays an encrypted message). On an iPhone (iOS 16.4 or newer) first add Saldo to the home screen ("Zet op beginscherm"), open it from there and switch push on: the card says so, and **installing signs you in once more**, which sends a "Nieuw toestel aangemeld" mail and push for your own phone. Push is per device (your phone and your laptop each have their own switch), e-mail per person. **Push needs `public_url` with `https://`**: without it the card says "Pushmeldingen werken pas als Saldo op een https-adres draait" and the switch stays away.
- Lock-screen rule: a web push cannot hide its text, because the phone's own setting (Android's lock-screen notifications, the iPhone's Show Previews) decides what a locked screen shows. Until the lock screens of real phones have been checked, **every push uses generic text**: "Planning: actie nodig", "Planning: let op", "Bankkoppeling verloopt binnenkort", "Gedeelde rekening: er is iets nieuws", with the line "Ontgrendel om de details te zien", and never an account name, a bank name or an amount. The details are in the app and in the e-mail, and a tap opens the right screen (a planning warning opens Planning on that month with that account first). A push cannot carry a colour circle per type or a chart: Android shows a monochrome status-bar icon and a large icon per tone (green, ochre, red ring), the iPhone the app icon.
- E-mails: the ten designed e-mails (login code, planning red, planning in the buffer, link expires in 14 and in 3 days, sync stopped, invitation, member joined, new password, new device) and the ones the design lacked, all in the same layout with a plain-text part: an invitation to a shared account, "Je bijdrage voor de Gedeelde rekening is er nog niet" (and "Partners bijdrage is er nog niet" for the manager), "8 transacties te categoriseren", the bank mails for the manager of a shared account ("Partners Argenta-koppeling verloopt over 14 dagen") and the week and month overviews with a receipt block. Member joined says whether the person linked the account at the bank or accepted your invitation. Every notification mail ends with a link "Meldingen aanpassen"; login codes, invitations, password links and the new-device mail have none (you cannot switch those off). The logo and the account icons are PNG images, so Gmail and Outlook show them (this needs `public_url`).
- Meldingen screen: eleven types in six groups (Planning, Bank, Gedeeld, Transacties, Overzicht, Beveiliging), each with a push chip and an e-mail chip. Defaults: rood, koppeling verloopt, synchronisatie gestopt, iemand sluit aan and uitnodiging push on and e-mail on; buffer and te categoriseren push on, e-mail off; bijdrage ontbreekt, weekoverzicht and maandoverzicht off and off; nieuw toestel push on and its e-mail always on (a locked chip). Besides the switches for push (this device) and e-mail there are "Hoe ver vooruit kijken" (1, 3 or 6 months; Start's planning alerts follow it too) and "Stille uren": no push from 22:00 to 07:00, a push that falls due then **waits until 07:00** and is not lost (the new-device message ignores quiet hours, e-mail never waits). When a channel is off its chips show off and stay disabled but keep your choice, which comes back when you switch the channel on. Every change is saved at once, and rolls back with a message when saving fails. On desktop the channels and quiet hours sit in a column left of the types.
- Planning warnings: "Zichtrekening staat rood in februari" and "Zichtrekening zakt in januari in de buffer", judged on month ends like Start's alerts, at **07:00** (the morning after the nightly sync changed the forecast), push and e-mail together. You get **one message per account, month and state**: a state that is still true is never repeated, an account sends at most one message per morning (red before buffer; the milder one follows on a later morning), a red month that improves to the buffer inside the same month is silent, and when the account is back to ok the month is re-armed, so a new dip for that month notifies again. Only accounts you can see, for the months you chose to look ahead, and not for a bank account whose newest data is older than 3 days (the sync message covers that).
- Bank, shared accounts and transactions: the 14-day and 3-day reminders and "Synchronisatie gestopt" now arrive as push too (before, e-mail only) and also go to the **manager of a shared account that the link feeds**, when no other healthy link covers that account ("Partners Argenta-koppeling verloopt over 14 dagen", naming only the shared accounts, never the owner's personal accounts; the manager can add their own link as a fallback). "Iemand sluit aan" goes to the manager. The invited person gets a push and an e-mail for an **invitation** to a shared account, a type of its own ("Uitnodiging voor een gedeelde rekening"). "Bijdrage ontbreekt" (off by default) runs from the 3rd of the month: the member who owes gets "Je bijdrage van € 436 is er nog niet" (or "€ 200 van € 436 is binnen") and the managers "Partners bijdrage is er nog niet"; a contributor without Saldo has no channel, so only the managers hear about them. "Te categoriseren" counts at **19:00** the new bank rows since the last digest that still have no category ("8 transacties te categoriseren": the number only, no merchant names) and sends nothing when there are none; CSV and Bilance imports and the old backlog never trigger it. "Nieuw toestel aangemeld" always sends the e-mail and a push to your other devices.
- Week and month overview (opt-in: both are off until you switch them on in Meldingen): the **week overview** goes out on **Sunday at 19:00** (the week Monday to Sunday that is ending, ISO weeks) and the **month overview** at **19:00 on the last day of the month**, one per person over all their current accounts ("Alle rekeningen samen · ter info", never a verdict). The push is generic ("Weekoverzicht: je bon staat klaar", "Tik om te zien waar je geld heen ging.") and opens a **receipt that prints in the app** (`/overzicht/bon/week/…` and `/overzicht/bon/maand/…`) with every category, its subcategories and their prices, what you saved, what is left, the comparison with the previous full period and, for months, each category against its budget. The receipt feeds out line by line and can be torn off (drag it from the grab strip at the paper's lower edge, or press "Scheur af"; with reduced motion it appears at once), and a finger on the paper still scrolls the page. The page has its own Week, Maand, Kwartaal and Jaar switch, previous and next arrows and the scope switch, and Overzicht has a small bill icon next to the account filter that opens the receipt of whatever period is selected (a month, a quarter, a year or your own range). The e-mail carries the same rows as a **static** receipt block (a mail cannot animate), labelled "Stand zondag 22 nov, 19:00". Nothing is sent for a week or month without transactions or when you switched the type off, and switching it on on a Wednesday never sends last Sunday's. At 19:00 the last day or two are mostly still pending and pending rows never count, so the snapshot reads low (the receipt says "Loopt nog"); the page itself is computed when you open it.
- **What changes for existing users:** the bank reminders and the sync-stopped message now also arrive as push and go to the manager of a shared account; reminders are checked every 15 minutes instead of every hour; a type you never touched follows its default (a missing choice is no longer "on"); **a reminder that was held back while its chip or channel was off is not sent later when you switch it on** (only the next one is), and a channel that cannot deliver (no push subscription on any device, no mail server) counts as off for that message; **signing a device out removes its push subscription** (also signing out your other devices, a password change or reset, and 90 days idle), so a signed-out phone never gets a push; when another person of the household signs in on a browser where someone else switched push on, that browser's subscription is dropped on the app's next start instead of being inherited. Known limits: the planning warnings judge month ends (a dip on the 1st raises nothing); a restore of an older database loses the notification log, so the next morning can repeat messages once; the first "te categoriseren" digest after you link a new bank counts the history that link back-filled; two members of a shared account with different look-aheads can get a message on different days; on a phone a long receipt prints in about 3 seconds and tears from the grab strip or the button, not from anywhere on the paper.
- **Fixed:** the account tile on "Planning instellen" (Zichtrekening, Persoonlijk, € 948 per maand) was squashed into a 34 px square with its text broken into single letters, because it shared its style names with the Instellingen page. Planning instellen now has its own style names.
- API: `GET /api/push/key`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/push/subscription`, `POST /api/notifications/test`, `GET` and `PUT /api/notifications` (the types with their effective push and e-mail choices, the e-mail and quiet-hours switches, the look-ahead), `GET /api/reports/receipt?periode=week|maand&van=…` and the public images `GET /mail/<file>`. The privacy suite covers every new route (a receipt never holds another user's accounts), and the notification tests check that a user never gets a message about an account they cannot see and that a manager's message about someone else's link names no personal account.
- For the administrator:
  - Push needs **`public_url` with https** (the log says "push notifications are off" otherwise) and the e-mails need the mail server (`smtp_*`). Demo mode runs no jobs and sends no mail or push (a fake sender and a stand-in device "Demo-toestel").
  - `saldo mail-preview --to <address> [--only <Function>]` sends every e-mail with demo values through the configured mail server, subject "[voorbeeld] …", to look at in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail; it reads no database and needs `smtp_host` and `public_url`.
  - `GET /mail/<file>` serves only the three PNG images of the e-mails (the logo and the two account icons), without a session; anything else under `/mail/` is a plain 404.
  - One background job, `notifications`, runs every 15 minutes and replaces the hourly reminders job; an hourly job, `push-prune`, removes the push subscriptions of devices that went idle.
  - The push key pair is made on the first start and kept in the database, its private half sealed with `secret.key`: a database restored without that key gets a new pair, and every phone subscribes again the next time it opens Saldo.

The Maanden cards show the **lowest point** of the month ("Laagste punt 1 nov · −€ 431,45") when it is lower than the end.

Each category row reads against its budget ("€ 295,21 van € 550,00 · 54 %"; without a budget the average of the last six months, labelled "gemiddelde"); here everything counts, also recurring costs, so Planning's budgets can show more room. A new **Saldo** card draws the balance per account with its buffer and the zero line, with the lowest point, the end and the days under the buffer.
- Overzicht: a tap on Inkomen, Uitgaven or Gespaard opens a sheet with the amount per category, and a dropdown per category shows its subcategories; every row opens the transactions behind it. "Per rekening" now says Uitgaven (Gespaard stays apart).
- Planning has three tabs (Kalender, Maanden, Budget). A cog opens the new **Planning instellen** screen with the budgets per category (− and + in steps of € 10, saved after a moment, with the live effect on the lowest month and "Terugzetten"), the recurring items and the one-offs; "Aanpassen" on a Budget card opens it too. The old `/planning#terugkerend` and `#eenmalig` links forward there.
- One-offs may have **no day**: leave the day empty and the amount is spread over the days of that month (in the running month: the days after today). The Kalender names such an item above the grid and shows no dot. Known limit: if you pay it during that month it counts twice until the month ends; remove the item once it is paid.
- Shared accounts: the Bijdragen card now shows on every shared account (also with one member). A **contributor without Saldo** (a name, an IBAN and a share) gets their part of the required monthly contribution and their payments are recognised by IBAN ("Bijdrage van Mama"). Only the person who added the contributor and the manager see the IBAN, masked; a contributor can be linked to a member who joins later, and only that member's own "Ja, dat ben ik" completes the link. A shared account cannot be switched off while it has contributors. Known limits: a link can only be offered to a member who may edit, a contributor's payment from another IBAN is not recognised, and a refused IBAN confirms to an editor that it is already used.
- API: `GET /api/reports/months?detail=1`, `GET /api/reports/budgets`, `GET /api/reports/balances`, `average` on the planning budgets, `month`/`wholeMonth` on planned items, `POST/PATCH/DELETE /api/accounts/{id}/contributors…` with link routes, and new `category` values on `GET /api/transactions` (`<id>-zelf`, `income-<id>`, `saved-account-<id>`). The drill-down of a saving category now lists only the rows the report counts as saved. A booked row dated after today no longer counts in the forecast's starting balance (it is part of the day grid when its day comes). The privacy suite covers every new route, including a sentinel that no answer holds a full contributor IBAN.

## 0.8.0
- Planning (version 0.8, plan in plan/v0.8-planning): see how each coming month ends for each account. Five tabs: **Kalender** (a daily balance chart per account with the buffer and zero zones, the month calendar, a day card with the end-of-day balance, and "Uitgave of inkomst plannen" with a live preview), **Maanden** (3, 6, 12 or 24 months as month cards with a breakdown), **Terugkerend** (suggestions found in your history to confirm, ignore (with "Ongedaan maken") or add by hand, and your confirmed items), **Eenmalig** and **Budget** (free to spend per account and month, budgets per category with the average of the last six months when you set none, an editor). Every account is calculated on its own: no sum carries a verdict, and the forecast is the same for every member of an account. **Take a Home Assistant backup before updating**: the update runs three migrations (0015 to 0017; one rebuilds the empty `budgets` table).
- Start: "Komende maanden" shows the month ends per account, and two planning alerts ("<rekening> staat rood in <maand>", "<rekening> zakt in de buffer in <maand>") judge on month ends; they open Planning on that month. A late expected item is dropped from the forecast after 7 days and marked "Verwacht, niet gezien".
- Saving categories: a switch "Telt als gespaard" on a main expense category (its subcategories inherit it). A row in such a category counts as Gespaard on Overzicht and Start, net, and wins over "Naar gedeelde rekening" and "Niet gecategoriseerd". The Gespaard tile on Overzicht opens exactly the rows behind the number.
- Declare an account shared: in Rekeningen (and on the account page, "Delen") the manager can switch on "Dit is een gedeelde rekening" and invite people from a select screen. An invited person who adds the account is a member at once (Bewerken, no bank link needed), and once someone has joined the account stays shared. Invitations expire after 30 days.
- Required monthly contribution: the contribution shares (percentage per partner) now take effect. The shared account's Budget tab and the Bijdragen card show what the account needs per month ("Nodig: € 872") and each partner's part ("€ 436"); Start's contribution row marks who is short.
- Overzicht sheets: a category row, an account row and the Gespaard tile open a sheet over Overzicht (a bottom sheet on a phone, a side panel from 1024 px). Every sheet in the app now slides in with a slow start and a small overshoot (a fade with reduced motion).
- New UI building blocks for the Planning screens: Tailwind CSS v4 (scoped, no global reset), React Aria Components and a few controls copied from Untitled UI React's free, MIT-licensed components into `web/src/ui/` (docs/third-party-ui.md lists each with its source and licence). The Planning route loads lazily.
- API: `GET /api/planning`, `GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE /api/planning/{items,planned}`, `GET/PUT /api/planning/budgets`, `GET /api/planning/suggestions` and `GET /api/planning/detection`, the shared flag and invitations (`/api/accounts/{id}/invitable`, `/invitations`, `/api/invitations/{id}/accept`), `category=saved` on `GET /api/transactions`, and `need`/`required`/`covered` on the contributions. The privacy suite covers every new route.
- **Before you rely on the forecast with your own data**, read `GET /api/planning/detection?account=<id>` on a local copy of a Home Assistant backup and check what Saldo suggests: the detection thresholds were tuned on the demo only (plan/v0.8-planning/02-recurring-detection.md). Rows without a counterparty IBAN, such as imported Bilance history, match an item with an IBAN by name and an amount within 10 %.

## 0.7.0
- Overzicht (version 0.7, plan in plan/v0.7-overview): see what you earned, spent and saved. Pick Maand, Kwartaal, Jaar or Eigen (your own range from a month grid with quick choices), tap a column to select a period, and read Inkomen, Uitgaven and Gespaard as tiles, with the categories below them: share, change against the previous period ("+12%", "nieuw") and a bar. With several accounts there is a "Per rekening" block; a tap shows one account. The month that is still running, and a first month that starts after the 1st, are drawn lighter and carry no trend. The filter, period and account live in the URL (`?periode=`, `van`, `tot`, `rekening`). There is no database migration; the update needs no extra backup.
- Every number opens the transactions behind it: a category row on Overzicht opens Transacties with that category and period as filters (`?categorie=…&periode=2026-10`), and the category dropdown of Transacties now lists the expense categories. "Niet gecategoriseerd", "Naar gedeelde rekening" and "Naar eigen rekening" are rows of their own. The rows of the list add up to the figure on Overzicht (a test checks it for every row of the demo and of a synthetic ledger).
- How the figures are counted: pending rows never count; split rows count by their parts; a refund lowers its category; a transfer between accounts of the selection is left out (so the € 450 contribution is internal in Alles, an expense "Naar gedeelde rekening" in Persoonlijk and income in Gedeeld); money sent to a savings account is Gespaard (net: a withdrawal lowers it, a month can be negative); transfers before the other account's history starts count as money that left. Known limits (imported history without IBANs, membership changes, foreign currency) are in plan/v0.7-overview/README.md.
- Start: the card "Uitgaven november" shows what went out this month, per account, with the same rows and numbers as Overzicht (including "Naar gedeelde rekening"), and links to Overzicht. In Alles a footnote says that transfers between your own accounts count per account but not in the total.
- API: new `GET /api/reports/months` (months only; the web app sums quarters, years and ranges, so changing the period needs no request) and a `category` filter on `GET /api/transactions`. A range over 50 years answers 400. The privacy suite covers the new route: Partner's accounts never show up in Bert's figures.
- CI also runs the speed test of the report in its plain (non-race) step.

## 0.6.0
- Shared accounts (version 0.6, plan in plan/v0.6-shared-accounts): share a joint account with a co-holder who links it with their own bank login, each seeing only their own accounts. Take a Home Assistant backup before updating: the update runs four migrations (sync report columns, join offers and notices, a rebuilt notices table, contribution senders); no existing data changes. The items below say what each part does.
- Sync diagnostics: after every sync, Rekening and Bankkoppelingen say per bank account what the bank returned and what Saldo kept (for example "de bank gaf 12 transacties vanaf 2 november, 0 nieuw (nieuwste 18 november), saldo van 18 november · bank-id …a3f9"), so an account that does not sync explains itself. The update adds six empty columns to the account sources table; no data changes.
- Join a shared account (0.6-1a): when you link a bank account whose IBAN is already in Saldo (for example Partner linking the joint account with her own bank login), Saldo no longer skips it but asks "Deze rekening bestaat al. Wil je aansluiten?", showing only the masked IBAN. Joining is tied to that bank consent: you become a member who may edit, see the account's history (never the other member's personal accounts), and the other members get an "… sluit aan" notice on Start that they can close. A rule of your own only applies to an account with one member, so the manager gets a notice and a "Je regels" card on the account to convert their rules to rules of the account. A pending offer stays on Bankkoppelingen for 24 hours. The joiner's link is kept as a fallback source and is not fetched yet. The update adds two tables (join offers and notices); no existing data changes.
- Members and permissions (0.6-1b): a shared account has a "Leden" card on its page. The manager sets each other member to Lezen or Bewerken (saved at once, with a notice to nobody: they see it next time). Every write now checks the right on the account in one place: a read-only member cannot categorise, split, add notes, change rules, import or undo an import, and only the manager changes the name, colour, buffer or balance of a shared account and the rights of the members. Before this, a member with edit rights could rename a shared account and change its buffer; that is fixed. The "… sluit aan" notice says whether the new member may edit or only read. No data changes.
- Lifecycle of a shared account (0.6-1c): any member can leave ("Rekening verlaten", the account stays for the others; when the manager leaves, the longest-standing member becomes manager; the last member cannot leave), the manager can delete a shared account for everyone by typing its name, and an admin can delete a user (Instellingen, Gebruikers; the sheet says how many accounts and bank links go, never which accounts: personal accounts of that user are deleted with their history, shared accounts stay for the others with a new manager). The other members get a notice for each of these. Removing your own bank link never deletes a shared account, and when it was the account's primary source another member's link becomes primary. The update rebuilds the (new, tiny) notices table; no other data changes.
- Primary and fallback sources (0.6-1d): a shared account is fetched through exactly one bank link at a time. The first link (the primary) carries it; another member's link (a reserve) takes over only while the primary is stopped: its consent lapsed or expired, the account is not shared in its consent, or its sync has been failing for more than 36 hours (before, any failed sync was meant to switch; now it waits until the account would read stale). The reserve fills the gap from the last stored booking, without duplicates, and pending transactions with their note and category move along; the primary carries again after re-authorising, or when it is tried again after 36 hours and works. An idle reserve never makes an account stale or raises an alert. Rekening says through whose link the account syncs, Bankkoppelingen marks a reserve link. A member only sees the error code of their own link. No data changes.
- Link time warning (0.6-1e): when you link your bank again and the bank consent leaves out an account Saldo already knew (for example you ticked only one of two accounts in the bank app), Saldo now says so right away: "Je hebt bij Argenta niet alle rekeningen gedeeld. <rekening> ontbreekt", with a button "Opnieuw koppelen", instead of silently skipping the account until the next sync. No data changes.
- Privacy suite (0.6-2): a test now walks every route of the server and fails when a new route has no privacy check; it fetches everything as each user and looks for the other user's account names, IBANs, counterparties, notes and rules, in five situations (normal, read-only member, member left, account deleted, user deleted). It found and fixed three leaks: the import history showed the id of a batch on someone else's account, an undo of a two-account import told a member that the file also covered an account she cannot see (she can now undo only her own part), and removing another user's bank link answered differently when that link was syncing. A second, independent review found one more: an unread "… heeft de rekening verlaten" notice showed the former member's current name instead of the name at that moment. Known and kept by design: the category tree is shared (anyone can rename or remove a category for everyone), an admin sees counts when removing a user, and joining a shared account needs no approval from the manager (the bank consent proves holder rights; the manager gets a notice). No data changes.
- Transfers and saved (0.6-3): money between accounts you can see is no income or expense. Besides the IBAN match, an opposite amount on another of your own accounts within 3 days now counts too (not when the IBANs say the two rows are unrelated). Rows are labelled "Gespaard" (to a savings account), "Naar gedeelde rekening" (from a personal account to a shared one) or "Overschrijving tussen eigen rekeningen"; the detail says what it means and has "Is geen overschrijving" / "Is een overschrijving" (and "Terug naar automatisch"), your own choice that nobody else sees, also for read-only members. A manual account with an IBAN shows "Overschrijvingen naar deze rekening". Detection is per person and never uses accounts you cannot see. The first page of Transacties is slower with this (about 0.2 seconds with 20 000 transactions instead of 0.015). No data changes.
- Scope switch (0.6-4): Alles, Persoonlijk and Gedeeld filter Start and Transacties (the choice is remembered per person), a scope without accounts says so ("Nog geen gedeelde rekening"), the sum of "Alles" is information only (it carries no state; every state and buffer stays per account), and the "te categoriseren" alert and the sidebar badge now count only the accounts in the chosen scope (before, they counted all accounts, so the link could open an empty list). No data changes.
- Contributions (0.6-5): on a shared account, each member says from their own side which account pays their share ("Bijdragen" card on the account page: pick one of your own accounts and press "Dit is mijn bijdrage", or, for a paying account outside Saldo, press the same button on the incoming payment in Transacties). Start and the shared hero then show "Bijdragen november · Jij € 450 ontvangen ✓ · Partner € 450 ontvangen ✓", and the payments are labelled "Jouw bijdrage" / "Bijdrage van Partner". The figure comes only from the shared account's own booked payments, so both members see the same amount; the ✓ means that something was received this month, not that the whole share was paid (that needs the monthly target of version 0.8). Nobody ever sees another member's paying account. The manager can set the split (default equal), which has no effect yet. Leaving, joining or deleting a member clears the claims and resets the split. The update adds one table (contribution senders); no existing data changes.
- Demo only: open `/api/demo/as/partner` to act as Partner (and `/api/demo/as/bert` to go back).

## 0.5.1
- Category icons are emoji now: choose one from a built-in grid with Dutch search ("auto", "huis", "kat"), or type or paste any emoji of your own (the phone's emoji keyboard works, a second emoji replaces the first). **The update rewrites icon values:** the 49 line icons of 0.4 and 0.5 become their emoji (the house is 🏠, the cart 🛒), any other value is cleared and shows a tag (🏷️). Ids, names, rules, splits and transactions are not touched. Take a Home Assistant backup first; the add-on also keeps a database copy before it migrates.
- Category colours: 16 pastels (ten new: Mint, Limoen, Turquoise, Koraal, Perzik, Lila, Oudroze, Zand, Olijf, Denim) and "Eigen kleur" for any colour of your own, in light and dark mode with readable text. Income categories can be recoloured too (they stay green until you change them). The update adds one empty column to the categories table; no existing colour changes.
- Choosing a category on a transaction is a two-step picker: a grid of main categories with their emoji, and a tap on one with subcategories shows its subcategories (with "Alleen Wonen" to pick the main category itself). "Toon inkomsten" and "Toon uitgaven" reach the other type. The split editor uses the same picker for each part. ## 0.5.0
- Learning and backfill (0.5-6): suggestions now learn from your Bilance history too (imported categories count as your own choices) and say how sure Saldo is. The certainty counts a few rows for less (two of two is 50 %, eight of eight 80 %): "Hoge zekerheid" offers "Maak regel" first, "Lage zekerheid" is only for confirming. New on the Suggesties tab: "Geschiedenis toepassen" shows what Saldo would categorise among your uncategorised transactions per counterparty (category, certainty, number of transactions and up to three examples), you tick the groups (high certainty is ticked, low is not) and confirm. Nothing is overwritten (only transactions without a category get one, each changeable on the transaction), no rule is made, and it is not undone by undoing an import. At most 200 groups and 2000 transactions at once. The update changes no data.
- Batches and undo (0.5-5): every import in the Importeren history, and the result right after it, has "Ongedaan maken". Saldo first counts what goes: how many transactions are removed (with their category, splits and notes) and how many of them you changed yourself, and that categories created by the import or taken over by existing transactions stay. Confirm and the file's transactions are gone in one step; manual and synced transactions are never touched, and the same file imports again in full afterwards. A file that went to several accounts is one entry with "Alles ongedaan maken". An import that added nothing (the same file again) stays in the history as "Niets nieuws toegevoegd" without a button; an undone one says "Ongedaan gemaakt op ...".
- Overlap and duplicates (0.5-4): the Controle step of a Bilance import now helps your old history fit with what the bank already synced. For each account it suggests a cutoff, the first date the bank synced (you can change it or choose "Geen afkapdatum"): rows from that date on are skipped and counted. Rows that look like a transaction you already have (same amount, at most a day apart, a similar name) are shown side by side as probable duplicates and skipped, unless you choose "Toch importeren" per row or "Alles opnemen". When such an existing transaction has no category, it takes the category from the file (never over one you chose, a rule or a split); the result says how many. The cutoff and the duplicates only apply to a Bilance file; any other CSV keeps the exact-match rule and its warning. The update changes no data model.
- Category mapping (0.5-3): the Categorieën step of the import wizard now maps every category of the file onto Saldo's categories. Each pair (Categorie, Subcategorie) goes to an existing category, to a new one (name, place and icon of your choice), or stays without; choosing the same category for several pairs merges them, and "Per hoofdcategorie koppelen" gives all subcategories of one main category the same target. Saldo suggests a category by name and shows it, and remembers what you chose per source: a second Bilance import opens with every known category marked "Opgeslagen". New categories are only made when the import goes through. Imported transactions get the category with origin "import", which rules and suggestions never overwrite; the check and the result say how many get a category. The mapping can be read and changed afterwards and re-applied to a batch through the API (no screen yet). The update rebuilds the empty category mapping table and adds two empty columns to the transactions table; no existing data changes.
- Import wizard and Bilance preset (0.5-2): Importeren recognises a Bilance export and fills in the columns, including the note, the two category columns and the account name. The wizard has five steps now (Bestand, Kolommen, Categorieën, Controle, Klaar): the delimiter, encoding, date format and decimal separator can be set by hand, a file with an account column (Bilance's `Rekening(en)`) maps each account name to one of your accounts and makes one batch per account, and Categorieën lists the categories of the file (mapping them follows in the next step of this version). Of a card payment only the merchant, date and place are kept: the card holder and the card number are never stored outside the raw row. A file may now be up to 20 MB and 100 000 rows (was 5 MB and 20 000). The update changes no data and adds no migration.

New synced and imported transactions get the category of the first matching rule; "apply" runs the rules over what is stored. A category you chose yourself or one from an import is never overwritten. The update adds two columns to the rules table and changes no existing data.
- Categorise in Transacties: change the category of a transaction in a tap (the pills of the main categories or the full list, a pending transaction too), tick "Toepassen op vergelijkbare en regel maken" to give the similar transactions that category and make a rule for new ones (it says how many change and how many stay: a category you chose yourself, one from an import and a split are never replaced), add a note (at most 200 characters, visible to everyone with access to the account) and split a booked transaction over several categories. A category you chose on a pending transaction, and its note, now carry over when the bank books it. Read-only members of a shared account see the category and the note but cannot change them. The update changes no data.
- Categorieen en regels (Meer): manage the categories and your rules in one place. Reorder the rules with the arrows, see how many transactions a rule matches before you save it, tick "Ook toepassen op bestaande transacties" to apply it to what is already there (a category you chose yourself, one from an import and a split are never replaced), and open a rule from "Regel aanpassen" in a transaction. Suggestions: a counterparty you categorised before gets the category you chose most often; "Bevestigen" categorises the transactions that wait now and makes a rule for new ones (on your personal accounts only; an equivalent rule is reused, and a rule for that counterparty with another category is left alone and mentioned), "Als regel" opens the rule editor filled in to adjust first, and "Negeren" dismisses it for good. Meer shows "N suggesties om te bevestigen". The browser's own dropdowns are replaced by one designed dropdown. The update adds a unique index on the suggestions table and changes no existing data.
- Category icons: every category and subcategory has an icon, chosen in the category editor on the Categorieen en regels screen (49 to choose from; the default categories start with a fitting one, a category without one shows a tag). The colour stays that of the main category. The update adds one empty column to the categories table and fills in the icons of the default categories; no other data changes.
