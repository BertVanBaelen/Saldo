## Channels
Push (Web Push to the installed PWA on Android and iPhone, our own sender on the standard library, VAPID key generated on first start and kept in the database, sealed; on iPhone only when Saldo is added to the home screen, iOS 16.4+) and email (SMTP from the add-on options). Each user turns each channel on or off and chooses per type. **The push switch is per device** ("Op dit toestel": this device has a subscription); the e-mail switch, the quiet-hours switch, the look-ahead and the per-type chips are per user. A user with no subscribed device gets no push whatever the chips say.

## Types and defaults
Keys are pinned (the mockup's `toestel` is `device`; the alert types of `GET /api/alerts` are another namespace). One defaults table in Go serves the API and the sender: a missing `notification_prefs` row means the default of this table, **not** "on".

## Rules
- **Look-ahead** for planning warnings: 1, 3 (default) or 6 months, per recipient. The warning is about what Start's alert shows: the **first red month** and the first buffer month before it, by the **month end** ([forecast](forecast.md), 0.8 decision 14).
- **Timing.** The planning warnings are evaluated once a day, at 07:00 or the first run after it, from the data of the nightly sync; push and e-mail both go then ("the morning after the forecast changes"). Other types go when they are due (a catch-up job every 15 minutes); the digest at 19:00; the **week overview** on Sunday at 19:00 and the **month overview** on the last day of the month at 19:00 (both opt-in).
- **Repeats.** The log holds one row per user, channel and message key; a message is sent at most once per key. Keys: account, month and state for planning; the connection episode for bank messages; the date for the digest. **Re-arm**: when an account's month is back to ok its rows are deleted, so a new dip for that month notifies again; a red month that improves to buffer inside the same month is silent. **At most one planning message per account per morning**, red before buffer; the other follows on a later morning if still true. Rows are written for every channel (`suppressed` where a chip or channel is off), so turning something on later never replays old states.
- **First run** after the upgrade writes the current states to the log without sending anything.
- **Stale data** gives no planning warning: a bank account whose newest data is older than 3 days (the sync message covers it); manual current accounts count.
- **Quiet hours** for push, fixed [22:00, 07:00) Brussels, **on** by default (a plain switch). A push that falls due inside them **waits** until they end; it is not lost. Security messages (`device`) ignore them. A test notification button.
- When a channel is off, its per-type choices are disabled and shown off, but kept: they come back when the channel is turned on again.
- Messages name the account: name, personal or shared, masked IBAN (`BE68 •••• •••• 4821`); the e-mail subject names it too. You only get messages about accounts you can see, checked again at delivery. A message to a manager about someone else's link names **only the shared accounts** it feeds, never the owner's personal accounts.
- **Digest `cat`**: counts rows that are booked, from the bank sync (not manual, not an import), booked at most 7 days before they arrived (a newly linked bank's backfilled history never counts), still uncategorised, with an id above the user's last digest; Bilance and CSV imports and the old backlog never trigger it; count only, no merchant names.
- **Contribution `contrib`**: required part of the running month's need (0.8: need × share, rounded up) against what arrived; once per account, month and member; not in the month the member joined, not for a share of 0 or a need of 0.
- **Overviews `week` and `month`** (0.9-3e, user decisions 23 Nov 2026): one overview per user over **all their current accounts** (Alles, "ter info", no verdict; the page can switch scope), ISO weeks Monday to Sunday in Brussels, opt-in (both off/off). The **push is generic** ("Weekoverzicht: je bon staat klaar", line "Tik om te zien waar je geld heen ging.", no amounts) and opens the receipt page; the **e-mail** carries a static receipt block with every category, subcategory and price, labelled "Stand zondag 22 nov, 19:00". Nothing is sent for a period without income, expenses or saved. At 19:00 the period is still running and today's bank rows are not booked yet: the receipt compares against the previous **full** period anyway and says "Loopt nog". Keys `overview:week:<Monday>` and `overview:month:<YYYY-MM>`, `Expires` after 3 days, first run baselined. The animated receipt exists **only** on the in-app page ([overzichtbon](../screens/overzichtbon.md)).
- **Push content.** Title, one line and the key amount (`−€ 1.033`) **only if** the lock-screen spike allows (below). Until then every push is **generic** ("Planning: actie nodig", "Bankkoppeling verloopt binnenkort", line "Ontgrendel om de details te zien"): no account names, no amounts, no bank names; one payload builder with two levels so the spike result is a one-line change. A push opens the app at the right screen (the planning link carries `rekening`). iPhone shows title and line with the app icon; Android a monochrome status-bar badge and a large icon per tone.
- **Push hygiene.** A subscription is deleted when its device is signed out (sign-out, sign out others, password change, 90 days idle) and when the push service answers 404/410. Endpoints are checked against an allowlist of push hosts (https only, no redirects, private addresses refused). On every app start the app re-registers its subscription, so a lost VAPID key heals.

## Lock screen: spike result
**Open** (0.9-2 step 8, confirmed in 0.9-5). Risk 14: a PWA cannot set the lock-screen visibility of its notifications; Android's lock-screen setting and the iPhone's Show Previews decide. Default until the phones say otherwise: generic text. The board's coloured circle per type, the vector status-bar icon and the expandable chart are not reachable from a web push (badge and icon are PNGs, an image is fetched without cookies and shows on the lock screen); see [pushmeldingen](../screens/pushmeldingen.md#build-notes-09).

## Email style
White card with the Saldo logo (PNG), a short label, the key amount or code in the type colour, an account block, a details table, one green button, and a footer explaining why you get it with a link to the notification settings ("Meldingen aanpassen", not on login, invitation, password and new-device mails). Dutch, with a plain-text part. Sender `saldo@<your-domain>`. Images are served from `<public_url>/mail/` without a session.
