## Goal
A push can reach the installed app on the Android phone and on the iPhone, only the devices that are still signed in, with a payload that says nothing a stranger at the lock screen should read, and we know what the lock screen really does.

## What exists (checked 18 Nov 2026)
`push_subscriptions` (0001: endpoint unique, `p256dh`, `auth`, `platform`, `device_id … ON DELETE SET NULL`), a hand-written `web/sw/sw.js` (cache only, versioned by `web/sw-plugin.ts`), `manifest.webmanifest` (`display: standalone`, icons), `NotBuilt id="meldingen"` on the Meldingen route, and `finance-mockup/img/statusbar-icon.svg`. Nothing sends a push. Devices are **revoked** (`devices.revoked_at`), not deleted, so a foreign key cascade never fires on sign-out.

## Steps
1. **`push` package (Opus).** `Sender` interface (`Send(ctx, Subscription, Payload) error`), `Web` (RFC 8030 delivery, RFC 8291 `aes128gcm` encryption, RFC 8292 VAPID with an ES256 JWT, all on the standard library; `TTL`, `Urgency` and `Topic` set per message so a re-sent message replaces the old one), and `Fake` (records what it was asked to send; used by every test and by demo mode, like `bank/fake`: CI and the demo never call a real push service). Errors: `ErrGone` for `404`/`410` (the caller deletes the subscription), anything else is retried on the next run. Tests against the **RFC 8291 test vectors** and a round trip through a decrypting test server. The VAPID `sub` claim is the public URL (https), not a mailbox.
2. **SSRF guard (Opus).** The endpoint is a URL the browser supplies. At subscribe time and again at send time: scheme `https` only, port 443, host on an allowlist (`fcm.googleapis.com`, `updates.push.services.mozilla.com`, `*.push.apple.com`, `*.notify.windows.com`), length cap, `p256dh` a 65-byte uncompressed P-256 point and `auth` 16 bytes (base64url), at most 10 subscriptions per user. The HTTP client of `Web` has its own transport: the dialer refuses loopback, private, link-local and unspecified addresses after resolution (no DNS rebinding), no redirects (`CheckRedirect` returns `http.ErrUseLastResponse`), 15 s timeout, response body read up to 4 KB. Tests for each refusal, including a host that resolves to `127.0.0.1`. `docs/security-baseline.md` row "SSRF" changes from "only Enable Banking and SMTP" to the list above.
3. **VAPID key and API (Sonnet, key store by Opus).** Migration 0018: a one-row `push_keys` table (public key plain, private key sealed with `secrets.Box`), generated on first start (also in demo mode, in `data-demo/`). Routes (docs/api.md "Push"): `GET /api/push/key` → `{key, enabled}` (`enabled` is false without `public_url`: a phone cannot subscribe without an https origin), `PUT /api/push/subscription` (endpoint, keys; the platform comes from the user agent; upsert by endpoint, bound to the **session's device**; `last_used_at` set), `DELETE /api/push/subscription` (this device's), `POST /api/notifications/test` (sends the generic test push to **this device's** subscription only; `409 "Pushmeldingen staan uit op dit toestel"` when it has none; at most 5 per hour per user). All with the CSRF and rate-limit rules of the other routes; `privacy_test.go` rows.
4. **Service worker (Sonnet).** `web/sw/sw.js` gets three handlers and a small test harness (`web/sw/sw.test.ts`, a fake `self`): `push` **always** calls `showNotification` (iOS revokes a subscription that receives a silent push; a payload that cannot be read becomes a generic "Saldo" notification), with `title`, `body`, `tag`, `badge`, `icon` per tone from the JSON payload `{title, body, url, tone, tag}`; `notificationclick` focuses an open Saldo window and navigates it to `url` (same-origin paths only, anything else opens `/`), or opens a window; `pushsubscriptionchange` re-subscribes and PUTs. New files in `public/icons/`: `badge-96.png` (monochrome alpha mask from `statusbar-icon.svg`) and `icon-info.png`, `icon-warn.png`, `icon-error.png` (192 px, the app icon with a green, ochre or red ring). The board's per-type circle and vector drawable are not available to a PWA (README known limits).
5. **Client (Sonnet).** `web/src/lib/push.ts`: `pushState()` returns `unsupported`, `ios-install` (iOS and not `display-mode: standalone`), `blocked`, `off` or `on` for this device; `enablePush()` asks `Notification.requestPermission()` **from a click** (on iPhone only from inside the installed app, iOS 16.4+), subscribes with `userVisibleOnly` and the key of `GET /api/push/key`, PUTs; `disablePush()` DELETEs and unsubscribes. **Self-healing**: on every app start with permission granted the app PUTs the current subscription, and re-subscribes when the browser has none or it was made with another key (a restored database without the VAPID key, step 3), so a lost key heals when each phone opens Saldo. Tests with a faked `PushManager`.
6. **The Pushmeldingen card on the Meldingen route (Sonnet).** Replace `NotBuilt` for `/meldingen` with `Meldingen.tsx` holding for now only the channel card from the mockup: "Pushmeldingen · Op dit toestel · Saldo op je startscherm" with the `Switch` of `web/src/ui`, the dashed "Stuur een testmelding" button (toast "Testmelding verstuurd naar dit toestel", or "Pushmeldingen staan uit"), and the two instruction cards: **iPhone** ("Zet Saldo op je beginscherm: tik op Deel, dan Zet op beginscherm. Open Saldo daarna vanaf je beginscherm en zet pushmeldingen aan. Je wordt eenmaal opnieuw ingelogd.") and **blocked** ("Pushmeldingen zijn geblokkeerd voor Saldo in de instellingen van je telefoon of browser."). [0.9-4](04-meldingen-screen.md) adds the rest on the same page.
7. **Subscription hygiene (Opus).** A subscription is deleted when its device is signed out, in the **same transaction** as the revoke. There are four revoke sites (checked 18 Nov 2026): `auth.SignOut` and `SignOutOthers` in `devices.go`, and in `tokens.go` the password reset (revokes every device) and the password change (revokes the others). The 90-day idle expiry is not a revoke in the database (`Resolve` only filters on `idleCutoff`), so the hourly sweeper below covers it. Migration 0018 also rebuilds `push_subscriptions` with `device_id NOT NULL … ON DELETE CASCADE` (belt and braces, existing rows without a device are dropped), and the hourly job removes subscriptions whose device is revoked or gone, or not used for 90 days. `ErrGone` deletes too. Tests: sign out a device and the fake transport never gets a message for it; deleting a user removes their subscriptions.
8. **The lock-screen spike (lead and user, last step; this is the old 0.9-5 moved forward).** **Gate:** the whole 0.9.0-dev build on top of the released 0.8.1 is on the live add-on (user decision 23 Nov 2026: develop it all, then test live; README decision 8: a deliberate exception to "no dev build on the live add-on"; Home Assistant backup first, rollback is restoring it because migrations 0018 and 0019 rebuild tables). The spike therefore runs together with the checklist of 0.9-5; until it has run every push is generic. Add to the spike: whether Safari reports `applicationServerKey` on an existing subscription (the self-heal after a lost VAPID key assumes a match when it does not), whether the `SameSite=Strict` session cookie travels on the service worker's `pushsubscriptionchange` PUT, and whether iPadOS is detected as an iPhone-like device. On the live add-on (README decision 8) send the bare test push to both phones with two payloads, **generic** ("Planning: actie nodig", "Ontgrendel om de details te zien") and **full** (an example with an account name and an amount). Record in the table below and in `analysis/concepts/notification.md`: Android with the lock-screen notification setting at "show all", "hide sensitive content" and "don't show"; iPhone with Show Previews "Altijd", "Wanneer ontgrendeld" and "Nooit", and Lock Screen appearance; whether the badge and the tone icon show; delivery with the app closed, after a restart, in battery saver; the latency. The outcome sets `notify.Detail` (`generic` or `full`) and decision 3 of the README is updated. Default without a decisive result: `generic`. If amounts on the lock screen are wanted anyway and the PWA cannot hide them, the Home Assistant companion app (`homeassistant_api`, private notifications) is the alternative and becomes its own phase after 0.9; it is not built here.
9. **Demo and docs (Sonnet).** Demo mode uses `Fake`: subscribing works, the test push answers ok, nothing arrives ("Demo: er komt geen melding aan" under the button). `docs/api.md` "Push" and "Notifications (test)", `docs/local-testing.md`.

## Tests
`go test ./internal/push/... ./internal/auth/... ./internal/server/...` (vectors, round trip, every SSRF refusal, upsert by endpoint and device binding, limits, the sign-out cascade, the test route's rate limit and 409); `npx vitest run` (push state machine, self-heal, the card, the service worker handlers).

## Done when
A test push arrives on the Android phone and on the iPhone from the live add-on, with the right icon; a signed-out device receives nothing; every refusal of the SSRF guard is tested; the spike result is recorded and `notify.Detail` decided.
