## Goal
One number per account: how much you can still spend this month.

## Steps
1. Formula from analysis 5.3: balance (or projected start) + expected income − fixed costs − planned − remaining budgets − savings and transfers still to come − buffer.
2. Negative shown as "Tekort".
3. Breakdown per line, so every number can be explained.
4. Month switcher for future months.

## Done when
Demo November gives € 365,44 for the Zichtrekening and € 46,86 for the Gedeelde rekening; February "Tekort € 1.933,47" for the Zichtrekening.
