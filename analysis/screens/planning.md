# Planning

Analysis: 12 (screen 5), 4.5, 5.3 · Design: Telefoon-Planning, Desktop-Planning · Mockup: `finance-mockup/planning.html` · Concepts: [forecast](../concepts/forecast.md), [recurring-item](../concepts/recurring-item.md), [planned-item](../concepts/planned-item.md), [budget](../concepts/budget.md), [buffer-and-states](../concepts/buffer-and-states.md), [account-group](../concepts/account-group.md) · Phases: [0.1-3](../../plan/v0.1-mockup/03-planning.md), [0.7-1](../../plan/v0.7-planning/01-recurring-items.md), [0.7-2](../../plan/v0.7-planning/02-budgets.md), [0.7-3](../../plan/v0.7-planning/03-planned-items.md), [0.7-5](../../plan/v0.7-planning/05-free-to-spend.md), [0.7-6](../../plan/v0.7-planning/06-planning-screen.md)

## Content
- Summary per group at the top (opens the Budget tab). The buffer links to Rekeningen.
- Tabs:
  - **Kalender**: month calendar, daily balance chart per group with buffer and zero zones (yellow / red), day card with items and the end-of-day balance per account, plan an expense or income. Sliding over the chart moves the selected day.
  - **Maanden**: horizon 3, 6, 12 or 24 months, yellow and red month cards, breakdown per month.
  - **Terugkerend**: detected items to confirm, confirmed items.
  - **Eenmalig**: planned expenses and incomes.
  - **Budget**: free to spend per group, month switcher, budgets per category.

## Design notes
From Telefoon-Planning (390 px) and Desktop-Planning (1280 px). Both boards share one model; only chart sizes and a few layouts differ. Mockup: `js/screens/planning.js`, `css/screens/planning.css`.

### Layout
- **Phone (< 1024 px)**: header "Planning" + avatar, scope switch full width, group summary (grid, one column per group), tab bar full bleed (margin 0 −20, 13 px tabs), then the tab content in one column.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024 px)**: header title · scope switch · buffer pill ("Buffer · zicht + gedeeld" / "Buffer · zichtrekening" / "Buffer · gedeelde rekening", amount, "Wijzigen" → Rekeningen). Summary cards in a row (max 470 px each, chevron). Tabs 15 px, left aligned. Kalender and Maanden: grid `1fr 360px`, gap 18. Terugkerend: grid `340px 1fr`. Eenmalig: max 660 px. Budget: group cards side by side, category cards in 2 columns.
- Page gap 14 on both.

### Group summary (top)
One button per group (Alles: Persoonlijk and Gedeelde rekening; otherwise the one group). Dot (group colour), name, free amount (Mono 18/600, `--greenText`, `--sharedText` for shared, `--neg` when short), note "vrij in november" / "tekort in november" (desktop "Vrij te besteden in …" / "Tekort in …"), a 6 px stacked bar. ### Formulas used by the boards
- Daily line: `bal(d) = bal(d−1) + items(d) − var(d)`, `var(d)` = already-spent variable / 18 up to today, remaining budget / remaining days after today, a full month's variable / days in later months. Start: balance on 1 Nov, later months the month-chain end.
- Month chain: `end = start + income − recurring (+ € 684 in January) − variable − saved ± one-off ± planned`; `left = end − buffer`.
- Free to spend: `start (today's balance, or the 1st) + income after today − fixed after today − one-off/planned after today − remaining category budgets − savings after today − buffer`.

### Deviations in the mockup
- "Autoherstelling" is filed under "Eenmalig en gepland" in the Budget breakdown (the board's test `note === 'Eenmalig'` put it under "Vaste kosten"; the total is the same).
- Remove buttons are labelled "Geplande post verwijderen: <omschrijving>".
- **[verify]** In Alles, the Maanden tab and the header buffer pill add both groups together (buffer € 1.500), as the board does. This conflicts with the rule that groups are never added together for a verdict (4.5, risk 16).
