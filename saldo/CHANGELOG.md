# Changelog

## 0.7.0
- Overzicht (version 0.7, plan in plan/v0.7-overview): see what you earned, spent and saved. Pick Maand, Kwartaal, Jaar or Eigen (your own range from a month grid with quick choices), tap a column to select a period, and read Inkomen, Uitgaven and Gespaard as tiles, with the categories below them: share, change against the previous period ("+12%", "nieuw") and a bar. With several accounts there is a "Per rekening" block; a tap shows one account. The month that is still running, and a first month that starts after the 1st, are drawn lighter and carry no trend. The filter, period and account live in the URL (`?periode=`, `van`, `tot`, `rekening`). There is no database migration; the update needs no extra backup.
- Every number opens the transactions behind it: a category row on Overzicht opens Transacties with that category and period as filters (`?categorie=…&periode=2026-10`), and the category dropdown of Transacties now lists the expense categories. "Niet gecategoriseerd", "Naar gedeelde rekening" and "Naar eigen rekening" are rows of their own. The rows of the list add up to the figure on Overzicht (a test checks it for every row of the demo and of a synthetic ledger).
- How the figures are counted: pending rows never count; split rows count by their parts; a refund lowers its category; a transfer between accounts of the selection is left out (so the € 450 contribution is internal in Alles, an expense "Naar gedeelde rekening" in Persoonlijk and income in Gedeeld); money sent to a savings account is Gespaard (net: a withdrawal lowers it, a month can be negative); transfers before the other account's history starts count as money that left. Known limits (imported history without IBANs, membership changes, foreign currency) are in plan/v0.7-overview/README.md.
- Start: the card "Uitgaven november" shows what went out this month, per account, with the same rows and numbers as Overzicht (including "Naar gedeelde rekening"), and links to Overzicht. In Alles a footnote says that transfers between your own accounts count per account but not in the total.
- API: new `GET /api/reports/months` (months only; the web app sums quarters, years and ranges, so changing the period needs no request) and a `category` filter on `GET /api/transactions`. A range over 50 years answers 400. The privacy suite covers the new route: Partner's accounts never show up in Bert's figures.
- CI also runs the speed test of the report in its plain (non-race) step.

## 0.6.0
- Shared accounts (version 0.6, plan in plan/v0.6-shared-accounts): share a joint account with a co-holder who links it with their own bank login, each seeing only their own accounts. Take a Home Assistant backup before updating: the update runs four migrations (sync report columns, join offers and notices, a rebuilt notices table, contribution senders); no existing data changes. The items below say what each part does.
- Sync diagnostics: after every sync, Rekening and Bankkoppelingen say per bank account what the bank returned and what Saldo kept (for example "de bank gaf 12 transacties vanaf 2 november, 0 nieuw (nieuwste 18 november), saldo van 18 november · bank-id …a3f9"), so an account that does not sync explains itself. The update adds six empty columns to the account sources table; no data changes.
- Join a shared account (0.6-1a): when you link a bank account whose IBAN is already in Saldo (for example Partner linking the joint account with her own bank login), Saldo no longer skips it but asks "Deze rekening bestaat al. Wil je aansluiten?", showing only the masked IBAN. Joining is tied to that bank consent: you become a member who may edit, see the account's history (never the other member's personal accounts), and the other members get an "… sluit aan" notice on Start that they can close. A rule of your own only applies to an account with one member, so the manager gets a notice and a "Je regels" card on the account to convert their rules to rules of the account. A pending offer stays on Bankkoppelingen for 24 hours. The joiner's link is kept as a fallback source and is not fetched yet. The update adds two tables (join offers and notices); no existing data changes.
- Members and permissions (0.6-1b): a shared account has a "Leden" card on its page. The manager sets each other member to Lezen or Bewerken (saved at once, with a notice to nobody: they see it next time). Every write now checks the right on the account in one place: a read-only member cannot categorise, split, add notes, change rules, import or undo an import, and only the manager changes the name, colour, buffer or balance of a shared account and the rights of the members. Before this, a member with edit rights could rename a shared account and change its buffer; that is fixed. The "… sluit aan" notice says whether the new member may edit or only read. No data changes.
- Lifecycle of a shared account (0.6-1c): any member can leave ("Rekening verlaten", the account stays for the others; when the manager leaves, the longest-standing member becomes manager; the last member cannot leave), the manager can delete a shared account for everyone by typing its name, and an admin can delete a user (Instellingen, Gebruikers; the sheet says how many accounts and bank links go, never which accounts: personal accounts of that user are deleted with their history, shared accounts stay for the others with a new manager). The other members get a notice for each of these. Removing your own bank link never deletes a shared account, and when it was the account's primary source another member's link becomes primary. The update rebuilds the (new, tiny) notices table; no other data changes.
- Primary and fallback sources (0.6-1d): a shared account is fetched through exactly one bank link at a time. The first link (the primary) carries it; another member's link (a reserve) takes over only while the primary is stopped: its consent lapsed or expired, the account is not shared in its consent, or its sync has been failing for more than 36 hours (before, any failed sync was meant to switch; now it waits until the account would read stale). The reserve fills the gap from the last stored booking, without duplicates, and pending transactions with their note and category move along; the primary carries again after re-authorising, or when it is tried again after 36 hours and works. An idle reserve never makes an account stale or raises an alert. Rekening says through whose link the account syncs, Bankkoppelingen marks a reserve link. A member only sees the error code of their own link. No data changes.
- Link time warning (0.6-1e): when you link your bank again and the bank consent leaves out an account Saldo already knew (for example you ticked only one of two accounts in the bank app), Saldo now says so right away: "Je hebt bij Argenta niet alle rekeningen gedeeld. <rekening> ontbreekt", with a button "Opnieuw koppelen", instead of silently skipping the account until the next sync. No data changes.
- Privacy suite (0.6-2): a test now walks every route of the server and fails when a new route has no privacy check; it fetches everything as each user and looks for the other user's account names, IBANs, counterparties, notes and rules, in five situations (normal, read-only member, member left, account deleted, user deleted). It found and fixed three leaks: the import history showed the id of a batch on someone else's account, an undo of a two-account import told a member that the file also covered an account she cannot see (she can now undo only her own part), and removing another user's bank link answered differently when that link was syncing. A second, independent review found one more: an unread "… heeft de rekening verlaten" notice showed the former member's current name instead of the name at that moment. Known and kept by design: the category tree is shared (anyone can rename or remove a category for everyone), an admin sees counts when removing a user, and joining a shared account needs no approval from the manager (the bank consent proves holder rights; the manager gets a notice). No data changes.
- Transfers and saved (0.6-3): money between accounts you can see is no income or expense. Besides the IBAN match, an opposite amount on another of your own accounts within 3 days now counts too (not when the IBANs say the two rows are unrelated). Rows are labelled "Gespaard" (to a savings account), "Naar gedeelde rekening" (from a personal account to a shared one) or "Overschrijving tussen eigen rekeningen"; the detail says what it means and has "Is geen overschrijving" / "Is een overschrijving" (and "Terug naar automatisch"), your own choice that nobody else sees, also for read-only members. A manual account with an IBAN shows "Overschrijvingen naar deze rekening". Detection is per person and never uses accounts you cannot see. The first page of Transacties is slower with this (about 0.2 seconds with 20 000 transactions instead of 0.015). No data changes.
- Scope switch (0.6-4): Alles, Persoonlijk and Gedeeld filter Start and Transacties (the choice is remembered per person), a scope without accounts says so ("Nog geen gedeelde rekening"), the sum of "Alles" is information only (it carries no state; every state and buffer stays per account), and the "te categoriseren" alert and the sidebar badge now count only the accounts in the chosen scope (before, they counted all accounts, so the link could open an empty list). No data changes.
- Contributions (0.6-5): on a shared account, each member says from their own side which account pays their share ("Bijdragen" card on the account page: pick one of your own accounts and press "Dit is mijn bijdrage", or, for a paying account outside Saldo, press the same button on the incoming payment in Transacties). Start and the shared hero then show "Bijdragen november · Jij € 450 ontvangen ✓ · Partner € 450 ontvangen ✓", and the payments are labelled "Jouw bijdrage" / "Bijdrage van Partner". The figure comes only from the shared account's own booked payments, so both members see the same amount; the ✓ means that something was received this month, not that the whole share was paid (that needs the monthly target of version 0.8). Nobody ever sees another member's paying account. The manager can set the split (default equal), which has no effect yet. Leaving, joining or deleting a member clears the claims and resets the split. The update adds one table (contribution senders); no existing data changes.
- Demo only: open `/api/demo/as/partner` to act as Partner (and `/api/demo/as/bert` to go back).

## 0.5.1
- Category icons are emoji now: choose one from a built-in grid with Dutch search ("auto", "huis", "kat"), or type or paste any emoji of your own (the phone's emoji keyboard works, a second emoji replaces the first). **The update rewrites icon values:** the 49 line icons of 0.4 and 0.5 become their emoji (the house is 🏠, the cart 🛒), any other value is cleared and shows a tag (🏷️). Ids, names, rules, splits and transactions are not touched. Take a Home Assistant backup first; the add-on also keeps a database copy before it migrates.
- Category colours: 16 pastels (ten new: Mint, Limoen, Turquoise, Koraal, Perzik, Lila, Oudroze, Zand, Olijf, Denim) and "Eigen kleur" for any colour of your own, in light and dark mode with readable text. Income categories can be recoloured too (they stay green until you change them). The update adds one empty column to the categories table; no existing colour changes.
- Choosing a category on a transaction is a two-step picker: a grid of main categories with their emoji, and a tap on one with subcategories shows its subcategories (with "Alleen Wonen" to pick the main category itself). "Toon inkomsten" and "Toon uitgaven" reach the other type. The split editor uses the same picker for each part. The long "Alle categorieën" dropdown is gone from the transaction; the Transacties filter and the import mapping keep their dropdown, now with emoji.
- Bank sync, hardening after the report that only one account of a link with two accounts synced (not reproduced; the cause on your live system is still unconfirmed): one account's failure can no longer stop the other accounts of the same link, a stored date with a time or a pending row dated far ahead no longer shrinks the fetch window of an account, and an account that the last consent left out is marked "Niet gedeeld bij de laatste toestemming" instead of silently going stale. Rekening and Bankkoppelingen now show why an account's sync failed ("Foutcode ... · laatste poging ..."). If an account still does not sync, that line names the cause.

## 0.5.0
- Learning and backfill (0.5-6): suggestions now learn from your Bilance history too (imported categories count as your own choices) and say how sure Saldo is. The certainty counts a few rows for less (two of two is 50 %, eight of eight 80 %): "Hoge zekerheid" offers "Maak regel" first, "Lage zekerheid" is only for confirming. New on the Suggesties tab: "Geschiedenis toepassen" shows what Saldo would categorise among your uncategorised transactions per counterparty (category, certainty, number of transactions and up to three examples), you tick the groups (high certainty is ticked, low is not) and confirm. Nothing is overwritten (only transactions without a category get one, each changeable on the transaction), no rule is made, and it is not undone by undoing an import. At most 200 groups and 2000 transactions at once. The update changes no data.
- Batches and undo (0.5-5): every import in the Importeren history, and the result right after it, has "Ongedaan maken". Saldo first counts what goes: how many transactions are removed (with their category, splits and notes) and how many of them you changed yourself, and that categories created by the import or taken over by existing transactions stay. Confirm and the file's transactions are gone in one step; manual and synced transactions are never touched, and the same file imports again in full afterwards. A file that went to several accounts is one entry with "Alles ongedaan maken". An import that added nothing (the same file again) stays in the history as "Niets nieuws toegevoegd" without a button; an undone one says "Ongedaan gemaakt op ...".
- Overlap and duplicates (0.5-4): the Controle step of a Bilance import now helps your old history fit with what the bank already synced. For each account it suggests a cutoff, the first date the bank synced (you can change it or choose "Geen afkapdatum"): rows from that date on are skipped and counted. Rows that look like a transaction you already have (same amount, at most a day apart, a similar name) are shown side by side as probable duplicates and skipped, unless you choose "Toch importeren" per row or "Alles opnemen". When such an existing transaction has no category, it takes the category from the file (never over one you chose, a rule or a split); the result says how many. The cutoff and the duplicates only apply to a Bilance file; any other CSV keeps the exact-match rule and its warning. The update changes no data model.
- Category mapping (0.5-3): the Categorieën step of the import wizard now maps every category of the file onto Saldo's categories. Each pair (Categorie, Subcategorie) goes to an existing category, to a new one (name, place and icon of your choice), or stays without; choosing the same category for several pairs merges them, and "Per hoofdcategorie koppelen" gives all subcategories of one main category the same target. Saldo suggests a category by name and shows it, and remembers what you chose per source: a second Bilance import opens with every known category marked "Opgeslagen". New categories are only made when the import goes through. Imported transactions get the category with origin "import", which rules and suggestions never overwrite; the check and the result say how many get a category. The mapping can be read and changed afterwards and re-applied to a batch through the API (no screen yet). The update rebuilds the empty category mapping table and adds two empty columns to the transactions table; no existing data changes.
- Import wizard and Bilance preset (0.5-2): Importeren recognises a Bilance export and fills in the columns, including the note, the two category columns and the account name. The wizard has five steps now (Bestand, Kolommen, Categorieën, Controle, Klaar): the delimiter, encoding, date format and decimal separator can be set by hand, a file with an account column (Bilance's `Rekening(en)`) maps each account name to one of your accounts and makes one batch per account, and Categorieën lists the categories of the file (mapping them follows in the next step of this version). Of a card payment only the merchant, date and place are kept: the card holder and the card number are never stored outside the raw row. A file may now be up to 20 MB and 100 000 rows (was 5 MB and 20 000). The update changes no data and adds no migration.

## 0.4.0
- Categories: one category tree for everyone, two levels, with the Dutch default set added once on the first start of this version (Wonen, Boodschappen, Vrije tijd, Vervoer, Abonnementen, Overig with their subcategories; Salaris, Terugbetaling, Bijdrage, Overig inkomen). Add, rename, move, recolour and remove them on the Categorieen en regels screen in Meer; removing asks where its transactions go (another category, which is also how two categories are merged, or uncategorised).
- Rules: categorise by counterparty (contains or equals), counterparty IBAN, communication (contains or a regular expression) and amount range, per account or for your own personal accounts, with a priority. New synced and imported transactions get the category of the first matching rule; "apply" runs the rules over what is stored. A category you chose yourself or one from an import is never overwritten. The update adds two columns to the rules table and changes no existing data.
- Categorise in Transacties: change the category of a transaction in a tap (the pills of the main categories or the full list, a pending transaction too), tick "Toepassen op vergelijkbare en regel maken" to give the similar transactions that category and make a rule for new ones (it says how many change and how many stay: a category you chose yourself, one from an import and a split are never replaced), add a note (at most 200 characters, visible to everyone with access to the account) and split a booked transaction over several categories. A category you chose on a pending transaction, and its note, now carry over when the bank books it. Read-only members of a shared account see the category and the note but cannot change them. The update changes no data.
- Categorieen en regels (Meer): manage the categories and your rules in one place. Reorder the rules with the arrows, see how many transactions a rule matches before you save it, tick "Ook toepassen op bestaande transacties" to apply it to what is already there (a category you chose yourself, one from an import and a split are never replaced), and open a rule from "Regel aanpassen" in a transaction. Suggestions: a counterparty you categorised before gets the category you chose most often; "Bevestigen" categorises the transactions that wait now and makes a rule for new ones (on your personal accounts only; an equivalent rule is reused, and a rule for that counterparty with another category is left alone and mentioned), "Als regel" opens the rule editor filled in to adjust first, and "Negeren" dismisses it for good. Meer shows "N suggesties om te bevestigen". The browser's own dropdowns are replaced by one designed dropdown. The update adds a unique index on the suggestions table and changes no existing data.
- Category icons: every category and subcategory has an icon, chosen in the category editor on the Categorieen en regels screen (49 to choose from; the default categories start with a fitting one, a category without one shows a tag). The colour stays that of the main category. The update adds one empty column to the categories table and fills in the icons of the default categories; no other data changes.

## 0.3.0
- Transacties: every synced transaction by day with the account colour and pending rows, search on name, amount and communication, filters for account, period and category, "Oudere transacties laden", and a read-only detail.
- Rekeningen: name, colour and minimum buffer per account, an account page with balance history, settings, sync status and recent transactions, manual accounts (savings, or a current account without a bank link) with a hand-updated balance and its history, "Rekening toevoegen", and removing your bank link while keeping or deleting the history. Removing a link waits at most 30 seconds for a running sync, then answers that it is busy and removes nothing.
- Start: the balance per account against its own buffer, Persoonlijk and Gedeeld sections with sync status, the sync button with progress, the alerts for an expiring consent and a stale or failed sync, and welcome steps that follow what you have done so far.
- CSV import (Importeren in Meer): upload a CSV, check the columns, choose the account, see the counts, import. A preset recognises the Argenta export; the file's own account and currency are checked, rows already in the account are skipped (also when the bank sync brings them later), and importing the same file again adds nothing. Every import is kept as a batch. There is no undo yet. The preset is built from the header only: check the first import against your real export.
- Fixes: "Onder de buffer" counts calendar days, an ambiguous amount in the search is ignored instead of guessed, "Deze maand" and "Dit jaar" show future-dated pending rows, and a link removed in another tab can no longer come back active.
- Sync engine: a nightly sync around 04:00 that catches up after a restart or a Home Assistant backup, retries a failed night with backoff, and syncs every account on its own. "Nu synchroniseren" and the first sync after linking run in the background; the web app follows them. A pending transaction is replaced when the bank books it, and a failed sync deletes nothing. The database copy kept before this update protects the one data change: older duplicate balances of the same day are merged.
- Reminders: e-mails "Koppeling verloopt" at 14 and 3 days and "Synchronisatie gestopt" (consent lapsed, or no good sync for 3 days), each sent once; a red alert on Start for a stopped sync.
