## Goal
Estimate the spending that is not fixed.

## Steps
1. `budget`: owner (user and scope), category, monthly amount.
2. Without a budget, use the average of past months.
3. Subtract what recurring items already cover, so nothing is counted twice.
4. Edit budgets in the Budget tab.

## Done when
Variable spending per month and group is available to the forecast, with budgets you can edit.
