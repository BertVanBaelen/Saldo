# Version 0.8 · Planning

**After this version you can** see how each of the coming months ends for each of your accounts, with the daily balance, the days an account dips into its buffer or below zero, and how much is still free to spend. You confirm or add recurring items, set budgets and plan one-off expenses and incomes. You can also mark a main category as savings ("Telt als gespaard"), so money you put aside counts as Gespaard even when Saldo does not recognise the transfer.

This answers the core question of the analysis: *"Will I have enough money at the end of each coming month, and how much is left or short?"*

Status: released as 0.8.0 (19 Nov 2026), verifying; refined 17–18 Nov 2026. Analysis milestone: M5. Roadmap: [../README.md](../README.md).

## Decisions (18 Nov 2026, plan refinement and the user's answers)
1. **Accounts are independent; no sum gets a verdict anywhere in Planning.** Every number with a state (month card, day, summary, free to spend, alert, chart zone) belongs to one current account. In Alles the Maanden tab shows one month list per account (the account name as a sub-heading), not the summed cards of the mockup; the desktop buffer pill lists each account's own buffer ("Buffer · Zichtrekening € 900 · Gedeelde rekening € 600"), never a total; a calendar day takes the worst state of the accounts in view and names them (as designed). This settles the **[verify]** in `analysis/screens/planning.md` against the mockup; the analysis's "Alles combined month ends" are dropped.
2. **The forecast does not depend on who looks.** `planning.Forecast(ctx, db, accountID, today, horizon)` takes no viewer. It reads only account-level facts: the latest balance snapshot, the account's own rows (amount, date, status, counterparty name, category and split parts), its recurring items, budgets and planned items, its buffer, and the category tree. It never reads `transfer_overrides`, `account_access` of other users or `visibleTx`. So every member of a shared account sees the same forecast, and 0.9-3 can call it from the scheduler. The access check is the handler's (`ledger` access as for every other route). Pinned structurally in 0.8-3 (the loader reads no per-user table) and on the API in 0.8-4 (Bert and Partner get byte-identical JSON for the shared account, also after Partner marked one of its rows "is een overschrijving").
3. **Variable spending is defined by categories, not by transfer detection.** The history the forecast learns from is: booked rows and split parts with an **expense top category that is not a saving category**, minus the rows matched to a confirmed recurring item of the account. Income categories, saving categories, uncategorised rows and transfers without an expense category are not variable spending. This is deliberately not `ledger.ReportMonths` (that rule is per viewer: an uncategorised transfer from the shared account to Partner's own account is `toOwn` for Partner and `none` for Bert). The two rules give the same per-category figure for one account whenever no row of that category is matched to an item; Overzicht keeps showing everything that happened, Planning only what is not already a recurring item.
4. **Free to spend is the month result.** `free(m, a) = end(m, a) − buffer(a)` for every month, the running one included (the mockup's € 365,44 = € 1.265,44 − € 900, € 46,86 = € 646,86 − € 600, "Tekort € 1.933,47" = −€ 1.033,47 − € 900). The Budget tab shows the same month as a breakdown (start, still to receive, fixed costs, one-off, budgets per category, savings, buffer); it is not a second formula. Tested as an identity.
5. **"Today" is the start, real history is the past.** The forecast starts at the end of today (Brussels, `clock`) from the latest balance snapshot plus every booked row dated after the snapshot's date. Days before today in the running month are the **actual** end-of-day balances, reconstructed backwards from that start; the mockup's "already spent ÷ 18" spread is replaced. Anything that should have happened by today but has not (pending rows, a recurring item up to 7 days late, a planned item dated today) is put on **tomorrow**, so today's balance is the real one. (It equals Start's "Saldo nu" when the bank's balance is dated today; when booked rows arrived after the balance date, Planning adds them and Start does not: see Known limits.)
6. **Only confirmed items count** (risk 12). Suggestions are computed on read and never stored (as category suggestions in 0.4-4); Bevestigen stores a confirmed item, Negeren stores an ignored one for the account. Items, budgets and planned items belong to the **account**: every member sees them, members with Bewerken change them, read-only members see no edit controls and get `403`.
7. **Saving categories (0.8-0a) are a report rule, written once in `reportBucket`.** A row or split part whose top category is a saving category is Gespaard (net), except an internal transfer inside the selection, which stays left out. Planning reads the flag too: saving categories have no budget and no variable spending; a recurring item of kind `saving` is the forecast's "Naar spaarrekening · Gespaard" line.
8. **Demo numbers: the verdicts of the mockup, the cents of the story.** As in 0.7 (decision 5) the story is kept and the mockup's figures are illustrative. With the seed of 0.8-1 the story reproduces every **state** of the mockup: the Zichtrekening dips below zero on 1 November until the salary, ends November and December above its buffer, January in the buffer (the yearly € 684 insurance), February red from the 10th (the € 2.000 car repair); the Gedeelde rekening stays above its buffer. The **amounts** differ for named reasons: Netflix (€ 13,99) is on the Zichtrekening in the story and on the shared account in the mockup; the 31 October balances implied by the story are € 1.023,55 and € 529,27 (mockup € 1.020,41 and € 646,86); November's real spending differs from the mockup's illustrative one; the shared Boodschappen budget is € 550 instead of € 650 (with € 650 the shared account would sit in its buffer every month, see 0.8-1). Golden numbers are in 0.8-3, derived by a throwaway script from the story plus the seed.
9. **No sums, no hiding of pairs.** The contribution's two halves are two items on two accounts ("Naar gedeelde rekening", kind transfer, on the Zichtrekening; "Bijdrage Bert", kind contribution, on the shared account); they are listed per account in every scope, also in Alles (the mockup hides them in Alles; hiding needs a pair link that would name a member's personal account to the other member). Item descriptions are stored as written and shown the same to every member (no per-viewer "Jouw bijdrage").
10. **The contribution shares take effect (user decision 18 Nov 2026).** The engine computes per month what a shared account needs: the month's recurring costs (expense, saving and outgoing transfer items, paid or not), the whole month's variable plan and planned expenses, minus recurring income that is not of the new item kind `contribution` and planned income, plus what brings the account back to its buffer from the month's start (`max(0, buffer − start)`). Each member's part is need × share / 100 (`account_access.contribution_share`, equal when `NULL`), rounded up to whole euros, shown next to what arrived (`received`, 0.6-5) on the Bijdragen card, on the shared account's Budget card and as the ✓ of Start's "Bijdragen" row. Engine 0.8-3 rule 12, API 0.8-4, screens 0.8-6. Demo November: € 872 needed, € 436 each.
11. **Package.** New package `server/internal/planning`: pure functions (key, detection, occurrences, projection) plus one loader that reads an account's facts with plain SQL. `planning` may import `ledger` and `categorise`; `ledger` never imports `planning`, so the planning alerts are merged into `GET /api/alerts` in the server layer. CRUD for items, budgets and planned items lives in `ledger/planning.go` (it needs `accessOf(...).edit()`).
12. **Expected but not seen** (user decision 18 Nov 2026): an occurrence up to 7 days late lands on tomorrow; after that it is no longer counted but listed as "Verwacht, niet gezien" (day card, Terugkerend, the running month's breakdown) until a payment with its counterparty arrives or its next date passes. Computed on read, never stored (0.8-3 rule 5b).
13. **Gespaard drill-down** (user decision 18 Nov 2026): the Gespaard tile on Overzicht links to Transacties with `categorie=gespaard`, the rows behind the number through the same `reportBucket` fragment (0.8-0a); from 0.8-0c the tile opens a bottom sheet with those rows and an "Open in Transacties" link.
14. **Alerts and 0.9 notifications use month ends** (user decision 18 Nov 2026); the dip on the 1st stays visible in the Kalender and the day card only.
15. **"Shared" is one derived value** (0.8-0b): `accounts.shared = 1 OR members > 1`, one column in `vis` and one Go helper, used by every personal-or-shared decision (scope, `kind`, `toShared`, `transferKind`, contributions, Planning's contribution need); "how many people" decisions (user rules, leave, hand-over) keep `members`. Invited users who add the account are members at once with Bewerken; before that they see only the account's name and who invited them.
16. **One sheet motion for the whole app** (0.8-0c): slow start, 2 % overshoot, 450 ms in, 250 ms out without overshoot, fade only with reduced motion; Overzicht's categories, Gespaard tile and accounts open bottom sheets on the phone and a side panel from the right on desktop (a deviation from the frozen mockup).

## Known limits (document, do not solve in 0.8)
- **Uncategorised spending is not forecast.** Rows without a category are in the balance (past) but not in the averages, so an account whose spending is mostly uncategorised gets too optimistic a future. The Start alert "N te categoriseren" is the remedy.
- **Transfers that carry an expense category** (for example a Bilance-imported contribution mapped to "Wonen") count as variable spending unless a confirmed recurring item matches them.
- **Weekends and bank holidays** are not modelled: an item is expected on its day (day 29–31 clamps to the last day of a short month, 29 February to 28 February in a common year).
- **Frequencies** are monthly, quarterly and yearly only. Every two weeks, four-weekly, twice a month and every other month are not detected and cannot be entered; the user enters such a cost as a budget.
- **Matching is by counterparty IBAN when the item has one, else by name** (`planning.Key`: normalised, digits and lone punctuation removed), within ±7 days and across month boundaries. A row without an IBAN (a Bilance import) matches an item with one by name and an amount within ±10 % of the item's: two same-name items whose amounts lie within 10 % of each other are not told apart on such rows, and an average item whose amount drifts more than 10 % loses its IBAN-less rows to variable spending. A bank that changes the spelling of a name-only item breaks the match: the occurrence looks unpaid (late for 7 days, then "Verwacht, niet gezien") and the row counts as variable spending in its category. The detection report shows it.
- **An item without a counterparty** (added by hand) matches a row of exactly its amount and sign within ±7 days; a payment of another amount is not recognised (it counts as variable spending in its category, and the occurrence is assumed paid once its date has passed).
- **Pending rows** of the last 14 days land on tomorrow (older ones are ignored), unless they match an occurrence. When the bank's balance is an expected balance (`XPCD`, chosen by the sync only without a booked one) it already holds the pending rows, which are then counted twice; the snapshot does not record its type, so this is not corrected.
- **Past planned items** are not counted (the balance holds them or they did not happen): one paid early is counted twice until its date passes (in the balance and on its date), one paid late is gone from the forecast on its date. They stay listed as "Voorbij" in Eenmalig until removed.
- **Alerts use month ends only** (user decision, reconfirmed 18 Nov 2026 after the review argued for the lowest day): a dip below zero inside a month, such as the 1st before the salary on the 2nd on an account without overdraft (a refused direct debit), shows in the Kalender and the day card but raises no Start alert and no 0.9 notification; chosen to avoid a monthly false alarm. A later option: red when below zero for 3 or more days.
- **Late items**: an unmatched occurrence of an item with a counterparty is moved to tomorrow for up to 7 days after its date, then no longer counted; it is shown as "Verwacht, niet gezien" (decision 12) but gives no Start alert or notification.
- **Required contribution** ignores money already on the account above its buffer (a surplus does not lower the need) and is per month, not averaged; a member's payment from an unclaimed sender is not `received` (0.6-5).
- **The averages use at most 6 complete months** since the account's first booked row; an account with less than one complete month of history has averages of 0 (the Budget tab says so).
- **Planning's "today" can differ from Start's "Saldo nu"**: Planning starts from the bank's balance plus the booked rows dated after it, Start and Rekeningen show the bank's balance as reported. They differ only when the bank reports a balance older than its newest booked rows.
- **Switching the shared flag reclassifies history** in every sum (as a membership change does, 0.7's known limit): a flagged account's past transfers become "Naar gedeelde rekening" in Persoonlijk.
- **Foreign currency** is summed as stored, as in 0.7.

## Real-data check (lead, after 0.8-2 and before 0.8-7; the lesson of 0.3-6 and 0.5-1)
Before the forecast relies on detection, run `GET /api/planning/detection?account=<id>` (0.8-2) on a **local copy of a production backup** (restore a Home Assistant backup of the add-on's `/data` on the development machine and start the dev build against it; never deploy a dev build to the live add-on) for each real current account, with 12+ months of Bilance history imported. Record per account: how many groups were suggested, which real recurring items were missed and why (the report gives the reason per group), which false suggestions appeared, and whether counterparty names of the bank and of Bilance give the same key. Adjust the thresholds in `planning/detect.go` (constants, one place) only with a fixture case in `TestDetect` for each change. Write the outcome in 0.8-2 under "Real-data check" with dates and counts, no amounts or names.
