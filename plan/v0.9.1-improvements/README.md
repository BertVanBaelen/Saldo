# Version 0.9.1 · Improvements after using 0.9.0

**After this version you can** open a subcategory of Overzicht in the same slide-in sheet as a top category, see the running month (and the running quarter and year) on Overzicht beside the complete ones, give every recurring cost a category, and set a budget per category as the **total** for the month: fixed costs plus what you spend on top. The Budgetten screen shows per category Vast + Extra = Totaal for any month, and warns when a total is below that month's fixed costs or below what you usually spend.

Status: **planned** (written 25 Nov 2026 on branch `v0.9.1-improvements`, cut from the released 0.9.0, main `c567f8a`). The user's requests and answers of 25 Nov 2026 are the decisions below; the plan was checked against the code of 0.9.0. Nothing is built. Revised the same day after the independent review ([REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md)) and the user's answer to its finding 1. Roadmap: [../README.md](../README.md).

3. **Recurring costs per category, budget = total** (IMP-3 to IMP-5, Planning instellen › Budgetten, per account):

   - Yearly and quarterly items count **only in the month they are paid** (the Planning screen shows big costs ahead; reacting is the user's responsibility).
   - **Both warnings, on the budget row only**: the total is below the month's recurring costs; the total is below the 6-month average.

   - Assigning an item **keeps the total and shrinks the extra**, previewed by the existing "Effect op de planning" strip.

4. **Assumptions of the analysis, settled here** (the user listed them "to confirm when planning"; the plan follows them, so they are decisions unless the user objects): only expense and saving items are assignable; an expense item takes an expense category that is not saving, a saving item only a saving category; deleting a category sets its items back to "Zonder categorie", a merge moves them to the target; contributions' need and the goldens change where budgets and assigned items meet (lead derives); a hint when an item's category differs from its payments' categories. ## What the analysis found (read against the code of 0.9.0, 25 Nov 2026)
- **Existing budgets change meaning.** A stored `budgets.monthly_amount` keeps its number and now means the total. Nothing moves until an item is assigned to a budgeted category: then that category's plan drops from `budget` to `max(0, budget − recurring)` in every month the item falls in. On the demo no item's payments sit in a budgeted category (items: Huur, Energie in Wonen; Netflix, Spotify, Internet en tv in Abonnementen, `server/internal/demo/demo.go:103-107`; budgets: Vrije tijd € 350, Vervoer € 300, Overig € 287,98 on the Zichtrekening and Boodschappen € 550 on the Gedeelde rekening, `demo/planning.go:47-53`), so accepting every suggestion changes no demo number. Assigning "Familiale verzekering" (yearly, January, € 684, no counterparty, no suggestion) to Overig makes January's Overig recurring € 684 > total € 287,98: extra 0, the January end rises by € 287,98 (lead derives the exact chain), and the row warns.
- **Two places hold a payment's category.** The item's category (Planning) and each payment row's category (Overzicht, `ledger.ReportMonths`). When they differ, Overzicht counts the payment in the row's category while Planning counts the occurrence in the item's. The hint ("Betalingen staan meestal in Abonnementen") and the suggestion make that visible; nothing forces them equal.
- **The 6-month average is per viewer, the forecast is not.** `LoadBudgetReference` reads `store.ReportMonths(viewer, …)` (`planning/budget_reference.go:56`), while `Project` and `LoadFacts` are viewer-free and pinned by `TestForecastLoaderReadsNoUserData` and the Bert = Partner test of `GET /api/planning` (`server/planning_forecast_test.go:175`). So that warning cannot live in the forecast (decision 11). `BudgetReference.In` hides the average once a budget applies (`budget_reference.go:95-99`): 0.9.1-4 adds an opt-in `average`.
- **`plan` has many readers.** `planning.Budget.Plan` feeds the spread (`forecast_project.go:545-566`), `Need.Costs` (`:662`), the wire (`planBudget(b)` is a struct conversion, `server/planning_forecast.go:224`), the Budget card and category bars (`web/src/lib/planningMonths.ts:135-138`, `:198-202`), the impact strip (`lib/planningImpact.ts:38`) and the budget editor, whose `step()` and draft catch-up start from `plan` (`pages/PlanningInstellenBudget.tsx:137`, `:205`, `:210-216`): left alone, a press would save the extra as the total. 0.9.1-4, 0.9.1-5a and 0.9.1-5b name every one.
- **A budgeted category with extra 0 would vanish.** `forecast_project.go:555` keeps a `Budget` only when `Plan != 0 || Spent != 0`: exactly the month the warning matters. Decision 12.
- **Shared-account contributions shift.** `Need.Costs` = the planned variable spending (`planTotal`) + every recurring cost of the month (`forecast_project.go:662-673`). With a total, a budgeted category with assigned items costs `max(total, recurring)` instead of `total + recurring`: the need drops by `min(total, recurring)` for that category. The demo's shared account has no budgeted category with an item, so its "€ 872 needed, € 436 each" stays.
- **Category lifecycle.** Foreign keys are on (`server/internal/db/db.go:46`), so `ON DELETE SET NULL` covers a delete without target. A **merge** (`ledger/categories.go:508-523`) moves transactions, splits, rules and budgets; items need their own step, and the target check compares type only (`:505`), so an expense item could follow into a saving category. `UpdateCategory` deletes budgets when a category gets the saving flag, turns income or moves under a saving parent (`:439-445`): items need the same clean-up.
- **Assignable kinds.** `recurring_items.kind` is `income | expense | transfer | saving | contribution` (`migrations/0017_planning.sql:22`); budgets exist only on top expense categories that are not saving (`ledger/planning.go:460-466`). Only `expense` items take part in budgets.
- **The suggestion needs the leaf category.** The forecast loader rolls each part up to its top (`planning/forecast_load.go:28-35`, column `tc`), and detection rows carry no category (`planning/detect.go:59-65`); 0.9.1-3 adds the leaf to the loader.
- **Seven columns at 375 px.** `barSize(7)` is `'s'` like `barSize(6)` (`web/src/lib/reports.ts:537`): the base `--bar-w: 10px`, gap 4 px (`pages/Overzicht.css:72`); the phone card's inner width (about 311 px) leaves about 41 px per column for two 10 px bars. Kwartaal goes from 4 columns (`'m'`, 18 px bars) to 5 (`'s'`). The lead checks it by eye.
- **Running periods need no server change.** `GET /api/reports/months` and `/budgets` already answer the running month (Eigen shows it, 0.7); the drill-down accepts `2026-K4` and `2026` (`web/src/lib/drilldown.ts:52-74`); the receipt takes a running period "as it is" (`lib/receipt.ts:146-157`). The running quarter's references cover only the months so far (the budgets answer ends at the running month); the running month counts its whole budget (no pro-rating, as 0.8.1-4).
- **A sub sheet is dropped today.** `parseBlad` accepts `categorie-<id>` and `categorie-<id>-zelf` (`pages/Overzicht.tsx:89-96`, `lib/drilldown.ts:105-125`), but the sheet looks only among the top rows (`Overzicht.tsx:731`), so a subcategory's `blad` is "stale" and removed.

## Known limits (document, do not solve in 0.9.1)
- Yearly and quarterly items count only in the month they are paid (user decision): that month's extra may be 0 and its row warns; the months around it keep their full extra.
- An item's category and its payments' categories can differ (Overzicht counts the payment's, Planning the item's); a hint shows it, nothing aligns them.
- The suggestion sees only the payments the forecast loads (the 6 complete months before the running month and the running month): a yearly item not paid in that window, and an item without a counterparty whose payment had another amount, get no suggestion.
- **A missed payment frees its part of the total** (decision 19): an occurrence "Verwacht, niet gezien" counts in its month's recurring costs but is not expected in the balance, so that month the forecast reserves less than the total (by the missed amount, down to the extra) until it is paid or no longer listed. The contribution need counts it, as before.
- **An "assumed paid" item can count twice** (review finding 3): an item without a counterparty is matched only by its exact amount (`forecast_project.go:328`); when its payment differs, the past occurrence is assumed paid (`:407-408`) and counts in `recurring`, while the payment row is variable spending in its own category (`:471-485`). In the same category the cost lowers the extra **and** the rest. Familiale verzekering (no counterparty) is such an item.
- **A late occurrence of last month counts in no month in view** (review finding 8): it is moved to tomorrow (`:405-406`) and paid from the running month's balance, but `recurring` goes by its own date, so the running month's extra does not shrink for it.
- A budget without amount (`null`, "use the average") has no total: the category keeps the variable average and its assigned items count outside it, as before.
- The 6-month average warning follows the viewer's report: two members of a shared account see different averages only when one of them marked a categorised row "is een overschrijving".
- A total set while viewing a month applies **from that month onward** and replaces the later rows (no single-month budget, decision 13): to cover one month's peak and keep the old total after it, the user sets the month and then the month after back.
- A category moved under another top keeps its items' category: their costs move into the new top's total (`UpdateCategory` deletes only the moved category's budgets, `ledger/categories.go:439-445`).
- The running quarter and year read their spending so far against the references of the months so far, the running month's in full.
- The "Per rekening" sheet's subcategory rows still open Transacties.
- The live preview of an assignment uses the item's stored amount; an item with the amount rule "gemiddelde" is forecast with the mean of its recent payments (W1's Gym: stored −€ 20, forecast −€ 25), so the strip after saving (the engine's answer) can differ from the preview.
- Detection suggestions carry no category.
