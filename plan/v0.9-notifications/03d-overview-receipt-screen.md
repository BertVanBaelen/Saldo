## Goal
The week and month overview as a **receipt that prints line by line** and can be torn off, with every category, its subcategories and their prices, in the app. This page is where the push and the e-mail of [0.9-3e](03e-week-and-month-overview-notifications.md) lead, and it is useful without any notification: Overzicht links to it, and it has its own week and month picker.

**The printer runs on this page only.** A web push cannot render React and an e-mail cannot animate or run script. The push stays generic until the lock-screen spike says otherwise, and the e-mail carries a static receipt-styled block (0.9-1, 0.9-3e).

## Decision: the component (user request 23 Nov 2026)
Use Kokonut UI's **Receipt Printer** (kokonutui.com), restyled and adapted, for the overview receipt. This **extends the 0.8-5a decision** by name: that decision limited Kokonut to small motion touches on the Planning screens; the receipt printer is a user request, for this one page, and does not open the door to other Kokonut components or to existing screens.
- **Source**: `https://kokonutui.com/r/receipt-printer.json` (Kokonut's own registry, no key; it is in the public repository's `registry.json`), one file, `components/kokonutui/receipt-printer.tsx`, header `@license: MIT`, v1.0.0, 2026-10-14, author Dorian Baffier, copyright (c) 2025 kokonutUI. The 21st.dev copy needs an API key and lists other dependencies (`next-themes`, `tailwind-merge`); not used. The file imports only React and `cn`; no `motion` package.
- **What it is**: a card with a printer housing and a slot; the paper feeds out a line at a time with short stops and a buzz, hangs from a hinge, can be pulled down (drag, or a sharp yank) and tears off and falls with a small physics step, with a serrated edge; reduced motion prints at once; Print / Print again buttons; an `aria-live` status line; an sr-only tear button. 24 KB, 789 lines.
- **What it cannot do as it is** (the reason for step 2): the card is a fixed `h-[30rem]` × `w-72` and clips the paper, so a tall receipt loses its totals (they are at the bottom); the paper is a fixed `w-56`; the paper is `touch-action: none`, which stops a finger on the paper from scrolling the page; the body is a hard-coded flat list with quantity × price, tax and a floating-point `toFixed(2)`; English strings; `dark:` and zinc classes that our scoped Tailwind (palette cleared, dark mode from `data-theme`) does not have.

## What exists (checked 23 Nov 2026)
`web/src/ui/` has `Tabs`, `Input`, `Select`, `Switch`, `SegmentedControl`, `DatePicker`, `Badge`, `cx` and `ui.css` (Tailwind v4 with the `tw` prefix, no preflight, Saldo's tokens); `docs/third-party-ui.md` lists the vendored files with source, licence and date. Overzicht has `?blad=` sheets with the app-wide slide, a Maand period and a category list with rows and subcategory dropdowns (0.8.1-4); `lib/format.ts` formats cents; there is no week anywhere in the web app.

## Steps
1. **Vendor and adapt the component (Opus).** `web/src/ui/ReceiptPrinter.tsx` with the source comment of the other vendored files (URL, licence, date copied, what changed). Changes, each an acceptance test (below):
   - `cx` instead of `@/lib/utils`; every class `tw:`-prefixed on Saldo's tokens; the `dark:` variants and the zinc palette gone. The printer housing and card use `--surface`, `--line`, `--text`, `--muted`; the **paper stays light in both themes** (thermal paper, dark ink `#27272A`), with its own soft shadow.
   - **Body as children**: `ReceiptPrinter` takes `children` (the paper's content) instead of `merchant`/`order`/`date`/`items`/`tax`; the hard-coded `Receipt` is removed. `merchant` stays as the housing label ("Saldo"). No `toFixed`, no `tax`, no floats: amounts are formatted by the caller from cents.
   - **Size follows content.** The card's height is `SLOT_Y` + the paper's measured height + a bottom margin (a `ResizeObserver` on the paper feeds the same `measure` the simulation already uses); the page scrolls, the card does not clip the totals. Paper width is `min(20rem, 100% − 3rem)` and the `marginLeft: -7rem` hack becomes half of the measured width. Long receipts print faster: the feed rate rises so a 45-line receipt prints in about 3 s, never more than 4.
   - **Touch.** While the sheet hangs, a finger on the paper scrolls the page (`touch-action: pan-y`); the **pull** starts from a visible grab strip at the paper's lower edge ("Trek om af te scheuren", `touch-action: none`) and from the mouse anywhere on the paper. A sharp downward yank on the strip still tears.
   - **A button for the gesture** (WCAG 2.5.1, a path-based gesture needs a single-pointer alternative): a visible secondary button "Scheur af" next to the paper, in addition to the sr-only one that exists; "Print nog een" is reachable by keyboard after the tear (the existing focus handling stays, with a test), and a tear is never required to read the receipt: the content is real text in the DOM, `role="document"`, labelled "Bon: week 47, 16–22 nov".
   - **Dutch strings**: "Klaar", "Aan het printen…", "Trek naar beneden om af te scheuren", "Scheur af", "Print de bon", "Print nog een"; the paper's `aria-label` from a prop.
2. **Tests for the adaptation (Opus).** Vitest with a fake `ResizeObserver`, `IntersectionObserver` and `requestAnimationFrame`: a 45-line body gives a card at least as tall as the paper; the totals are in the DOM and visible after printing; `prefers-reduced-motion` shows the whole receipt at once; the "Scheur af" button tears (calls `onTear`, the paper leaves, "Print nog een" gets focus and prints again); a pointer-down on the paper body does not start a drag, on the strip it does; no class without the `tw:` prefix and no `dark:` in the compiled CSS (extend the smoke test of `ui.test.tsx`).
3. **The page (Sonnet).** Route `/overzicht/bon/:periode/:van` (`periode` `week` or `maand`, `van` a date, lazy-loaded; nav item inside Overzicht's tab parent; `*` and an unknown period fall to `NotBuilt`/a not-found as the app does). Loads `GET /api/reports/receipt` with the app-wide scope (the notifications' link carries `?scope=alles`, which wins **for this page only**: it never changes the app-wide scope, so opening a push does not switch the user's Overzicht to Alles). Header: back chevron to Overzicht, title "Overzichtsbon", a `SegmentedControl` Week · Maand, previous and next arrows (back to the history's first period, forward to the running one; labelled "Vorige week", "Volgende maand"), and the scope switch of Overzicht. Below the printer: the link "Open in Overzicht" (months: `/overzicht?periode=maand&…`; weeks have no Overzicht equivalent, so the link is "Bekijk de transacties" with a date-range drill-down) and the model's notes as a plain list under the card, **also** printed on the paper, so a screen reader reading the paper hears them. Loading: a ghost paper; error: the usual error card; `empty`: "Geen transacties in deze week." with the next and previous arrows still working. The URL carries the period so Back and a reload work; changing the period replaces, not pushes.
4. **The paper's body (Sonnet).** `ReceiptBody.tsx`, monospace (Geist Mono, 11 px, 15 px lines, the line height the simulation assumes), top to bottom as a receipt: `SALDO`, the period label, the scope label ("Alle rekeningen samen · ter info"), a dotted rule, then **INKOMEN** (total, one indented row per income category), **UITGAVEN** (total with the trend), each category as a row with its amount and trend and, indented, its subcategories with amounts (the own part with the wording of `breakdown`); on months a second line under a category with a budget: "budget 550,00 · 117 %" or "gemiddeld 520,00 · 54 %"; "Niet gecategoriseerd" and the transfer rows in the same largest-first order as Overzicht's list; **GESPAARD**; a dotted rule; **OVER** (the result) with the previous period's result under it; the notes; a decorative barcode (`aria-hidden`) and a footer line ("Tot volgende week" / "Tot volgende maand"). Amounts as `1.234,56` (the app's formatter, U+2212 minus, no currency symbol per line, a line "bedragen in €" under the rule), names truncated with an ellipsis and the full text in `title`, rows as `<p>` with two spans like the original. Trends as text ("+12 %", "−7 %", "= 0 %", "nieuw"), in the one ink colour. Dutch throughout; no verdict words ("rood", "te veel").
5. **The entry from Overzicht (Sonnet; changed 25 Nov 2026 at the user's request).** A small bill icon (a link, glyph only, labelled "Bekijk de bon van <periode>") right of the account filter chip, for EVERY period of Overzicht (Maand, Kwartaal, Jaar, Eigen), linking to `/overzicht/bon/<kind>/<first day>?scope=<current>[&rekening=<id>]` (Eigen: `/eigen/<first day>?tot=<last day>`); it replaces the first design, a "Bekijk als bon" button in the category card's header on the Maand view. The receipt gained the kinds `kwartaal`, `jaar` and `eigen` for it (server: `receipt.Quarter`, `Year`, `Custom`; budgets stay months-only). No week entry in Overzicht (it has no week period); weeks are reached from the bon page and from the notification. `analysis/screens/overzicht.md` gets the deviation line.
6. **Docs (Sonnet).** `docs/third-party-ui.md`: the row for `ReceiptPrinter.tsx` (source, MIT, copied 23 Nov 2026, the list of changes above), `docs/architecture.md` one line, `docs/local-testing.md` section "Overzichtsbon (0.9)".
7. **Real phone and the demo (lead).** At 375 px and desktop, light and dark; reduced motion on; a **40-line receipt on a real phone**: a finger on the paper scrolls the page, the grab strip tears, the button tears, the page does not jump when the paper falls; keyboard only; a screen reader reads the paper top to bottom without the printing animation getting in the way. Bundle size delta measured and reported (the route is lazy; the printer is about 8 KB minified).

## Tests
`npx vitest run` (step 2; `ReceiptBody` renders every row kind: income, a category with and without children, a budget line, the transfer rows, notes, the empty state; the Dutch money format; the route's period handling, the arrows' limits, scope from the URL; the Overzicht button).

## Done when
The week and the month of the demo print as receipts with every category, subcategory and price; the totals are never clipped; the page scrolls on a phone with a finger on the paper; the tear works by drag and by button; reduced motion shows the receipt at once; the vendored component is listed with its source and licence; the existing screens are unchanged.
