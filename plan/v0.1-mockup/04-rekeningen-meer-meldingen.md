## Goal
The settings side of the app: accounts, the phone "Meer" menu and notification preferences.

## Steps
1. Rekeningen: buffer overview, accounts per scope, settings sheet (phone) or panel (desktop) with name, 8 colours and minimum buffer.
2. Meer: profile, Geld and App sections, Weergave (Systeem · Licht · Donker), Afmelden. Desktop layout derived.
3. Meldingen: channel switches, push / e-mail per type, look-ahead, quiet hours, test notification. Desktop layout derived.

## Done when
All three screens match the boards on phone; the derived desktop layouts follow the same style. ✔
