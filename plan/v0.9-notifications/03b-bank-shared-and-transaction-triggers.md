## Goal
Every other type of the Meldingen screen is sent, to the right people only, once, and without telling anyone more than they may see.

## No migration
Every trigger keys its repeat protection in `notification_log` (`notice:<id>`, `invitation:<id>`, `connection:…`, `digest:<date>`, `contrib:<account>|<month>|<member>`); nothing new in the schema. The baselines are markers in `notify_runs` (0.9-3 step 6), written on each trigger's own first run. (If a step finds it cannot do without a column, it adds migration 0022 (0.8.1 took 0020 and 0021) and says why here.)

## Steps
1. **Bank messages move to the dispatcher with new recipients (README decision 5).** `consent` (14 and 3 days) and `stop` (lapsed or failing) keep their episode keys. Recipients per connection: the **owner** (as today) and the **manager of every shared account that this connection feeds, when no other healthy source covers that account** (another source on an active connection whose status is ok; the logic of `coveredBy` in `ledger/alerts.go`, risk 5b). The manager gets the **manager variant** (0.9-1 row 14): "Partners Argenta-koppeling verloopt over 14 dagen", naming only the **shared** accounts the link feeds, never the owner's personal accounts or IBANs; button "Bekijk de rekening" (a manager cannot re-link someone else's consent), note about adding their own link as a fallback. When the manager is the owner there is one message. Their own preferences decide the channels. Tests: the manager's mail and push contain no personal account name or IBAN of the owner; a healthy fallback source silences the manager; two shared accounts fed by one link are one message.
2. **"Iemand sluit aan" (`joined`).** Source: `account_notices` rows of type `member` (written by `addMember`, one per other member, `actor_id` = the joiner), created within the last 3 days (`Expires`), key `notice:<id>`. Only the **manager of the account at send time** is told (the other members' rows stay in-app only); a notice whose account is gone or whose recipient is no longer its manager is dropped. The e-mail variant is derived at send time: the joiner has an own source on the account → "heeft de rekening ook gekoppeld", otherwise "heeft je uitnodiging aangenomen"; the role row says "mag bewerken" (every joiner has Bewerken today). Test through both paths (`JoinByInvitation` and `AcceptInvitation`) and the join offer.
3. **Invitation received (`invite`).** Source: open `account_invitations` rows (0.8-0b) younger than 3 days, key `invitation:<id>`; recipient the invited user; push title generic "Gedeelde rekening: er is iets nieuws", e-mail `SharedInvitation`. A withdrawn or accepted invitation (row gone) sends nothing, also when it was still waiting for quiet hours to end.
4. **"Te categoriseren" digest (`cat`, README decision 7).** A daily trigger, due from 19:00 (Brussels) once per day (`notify_runs`). Per user: the count of rows that are **new** since the user's last digest (`id` above the high-water mark kept in the `state` of the `digest:<date>` rows), **booked and from the bank sync** (`source_connection_id IS NOT NULL AND import_batch_id IS NULL`; manual rows, Bilance and CSV imports never count), still uncategorised by the same condition as `ledger.UncategorisedCount` (a `sinceID` variant of it), on accounts the user can see, all scopes. Count 0: nothing is written and the mark stays. Count above 0: one message ("8 transacties te categoriseren", link `/transacties?filter=niet-gecategoriseerd`, no merchant names), the row is written for every channel (suppressed where the chip is off) and the mark moves to the highest id, so the backlog never repeats. The digest's own baseline (`baseline:digest`) sets the first mark on its first run. Tests: an import of 500 rows sends nothing; the same rows categorised by a rule before 19:00 send nothing; a row that arrives after the mark counts once.
5. **"Bijdrage ontbreekt" (`contrib`, README decision 6).** A daily trigger from 07:00, acting from the 3rd of the month. For each shared account with at least two members and a running month `Need` above 0 (`planning.Forecast`, `Months[0].Need`): each member's **required** part (`memberPart` of `server/contributions.go`, moved into `planning` as a pure function, with the shares of `account_access.contribution_share`) against what **arrived** this month. The account-level part of `ledger.Contributions` (members, shares, received, no viewer) is extracted into a function the viewer-scoped method calls; the lead checks the extraction. A member with `received` below `required` gets "Je bijdrage van € 436 is er nog niet" (partial: "€ 200 van € 436 is binnen") and every **manager other than that member** gets "Partners bijdrage is er nog niet"; key `contrib:<account>|<month>|<member>`, once per month, default off/off so most rows are `suppressed`. Not sent: in the month the member joined, for a share of 0, when the need is 0, when the member left, for an account with one member. The messages name the account and the month and **no personal account**. Tests with `planningWorld`: the demo's € 436 each, a partial payment, a 70/30 split, the claim of a sender between the 3rd and the 4th (nothing is sent after it is paid).
6. **New device (`device`).** In `postVerify` the direct `sendLater(mail.NewDevice(…))` becomes a `Deliver` of a `Security` message (quiet hours ignored, e-mail always on, key `device:<device id>`), run in the background like `sendLater` so the sign-in answer and its timing do not change (the no-enumeration rule of the security baseline). The **push** goes to the user's other subscriptions (the new device has none yet); title "Nieuw toestel aangemeld" and, at detail `full`, the device name. Login code, invitation and password-reset mails stay direct. The installed iPhone app counts as a new device (README): documented, not special-cased.
7. **Left, manager changed, deleted, rules** stay in-app notices: no push, no e-mail, no type on the Meldingen screen. One line in `analysis/concepts/notification.md`.
8. **Wiring and tests.** Register the triggers in `notify.Run` after the planning trigger (the order of 0.9-3 step 7); add the simulated-month scenario of 0.9-3 with a link that lapses (owner Partner, manager Bert), a join, an invitation, a contribution that arrives late and a CSV import. Golden messages listed here when derived. `privacy_test.go`: the sentinels (a user never gets a message about an account they cannot see; the manager's bank message names no personal account of the owner).

## Done when
Each type produces exactly its expected messages over the simulated month, to the right people, nothing twice across a restart; a manager is told when a link they depend on lapses, without learning anything about the owner's personal accounts; an import does not trigger the digest.

## Note from 0.8.1 (20 Nov 2026)
- Version 0.8.1 added **contributors without Saldo** on shared accounts (`contributors` table, migration 0020; shares keyed `c<id>`; `ledger.Contributions` and `GET /api/planning` list them next to the members). The "Bijdrage ontbreekt" trigger (step 5) must cover them too: a contributor with `received` below `required` has no channel, so **only the managers** are notified ("<naam>s bijdrage is er nog niet"), key `contrib:<account>|<month>|c<id>`. The required part is `memberPart` over members plus contributors.
- Migration numbers: 0.8.1 took **0020** (contributors) and **0021** (one-off without a day). 0.9 keeps 0018 and 0019 (the migrator applies every version not yet recorded, gaps allowed); a further 0.9 migration would be 0022.
- 0.9-1 e-mail wording for planning months should follow the app's new month wording (a result chip "+€ 183" or "−€ 200", state label "in de buffer" / "rood", the lowest point shown separately).
