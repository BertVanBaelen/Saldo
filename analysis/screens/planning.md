## Content
- Summary per group at the top (opens the Budget tab). The buffer links to Rekeningen.
- Three tabs (0.8.1-8; before there were five):
  - **Kalender**: month calendar, daily balance chart per group with buffer and zero zones (yellow / red), day card with items and the end-of-day balance per account, plan an expense or income. Sliding over the chart moves the selected day.
  - **Maanden**: horizon 3, 6, 12 or 24 months, yellow and red month cards, breakdown per month.
  - **Budget**: free to spend per group, month switcher, budgets per category (read-only here; "Aanpassen" opens Planning instellen).
- A cog at the top right (`aria-label` "Planning instellen") opens the screen **Planning instellen** (`/planning/instellen`, below): the budgets, the recurring items ("Terugkerend") and the one-off items ("Eenmalig") of one account. Planning stays selected in the sidebar and the phone tab bar.

## Design notes
From Telefoon-Planning (390 px) and Desktop-Planning (1280 px). Both boards share one model; only chart sizes and a few layouts differ. Mockup: `js/screens/planning.js`, `css/screens/planning.css`.

### Formulas (as built in 0.8; the boards' own formulas differ where noted)
- Daily line: days before today in the running month are the **actual** end-of-day balances (from the latest bank balance backwards); from tomorrow on `bal(d) = bal(d−1) + items(d) − var(d)`, where `var(d)` is the rest of the month's budgets spread over the remaining days (running month) or the month's budgets and averages spread over all days (later months). The boards spread "already spent ÷ 18" over past days instead; that was a stand-in for real rows. Anything due by today that has not happened yet (pending rows, a recurring item up to 7 days late, a plan for today) lands on tomorrow. Plan 0.8-3.
- Month chain: `end = start + recurring income − recurring costs (with yearly and quarterly items in their month) − variable − saved ± planned`; `left = end − buffer`.
- Free to spend = `left` of that month, for the running month as well (the boards' formula gives the same number: € 365,44 = € 1.265,44 − € 900). The Budget tab shows it as a breakdown.

### Deviations in the mockup
- "Autoherstelling" is filed under "Eenmalig en gepland" in the Budget breakdown (the board's test `note === 'Eenmalig'` put it under "Vaste kosten"; the total is the same).
- Remove buttons are labelled "Geplande post verwijderen: <omschrijving>".
