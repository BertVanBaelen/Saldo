## Goal
See your real transactions and manage your accounts in the app.

## Steps
1. Transacties on real data: day groups, account labels, search, pending rows, detail (read-only until 0.4).
2. Rekeningen: name, colour (8) and minimum buffer per account.
3. Manual accounts (savings): create, register an IBAN for transfer recognition, update the balance by hand with history and "last updated".
4. Sync button and per-account sync status on Start.

## Done when
You see your synced transactions and your savings accounts, and the buffer you set is stored per account.
