## Goal
See whether both partners paid their part into the shared account.

## Steps
1. A rule labels incoming payments from a member as a contribution.
2. Split per member as a fixed percentage, default equal, set by the manager.
3. "Bijdragen november · Jij € 450 ✓ · Partner € 450 ✓" on Start and in the shared hero.
4. Required monthly contribution once the forecast exists (0.7-4).

## Done when
Both contributions show as received on Start in the month they arrive.
