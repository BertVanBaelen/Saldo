## Goal
Add the expenses and incomes you know are coming.

## Steps
1. `planned_item`: account, created by, date, signed amount, description.
2. Plan from the calendar: expense or income, account (chosen when several are in view), end-of-day balance preview.
3. Eenmalig list; edit and remove.

## Done when
A planned item shows in the calendar, the Eenmalig list, the budget and the forecast, and can be edited and removed.
