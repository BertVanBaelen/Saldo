## Goal
The Planning screen from the mockup on real data, and the forecast on Start.

## Steps
1. Summary per account and the five tabs: Kalender, Maanden (per account), Terugkerend, Eenmalig, Budget, with the edit forms as designed.
2. Daily balance chart with zones and day selection by drag and keyboard.
3. Start: "Komende maanden" bars per account and the planning alerts (red and buffer), naming the account.
4. Buffer link to Rekeningen; read-only members see no edit controls.

## Done when
