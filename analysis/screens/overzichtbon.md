# Overzichtsbon (receipt of a week, month, quarter, year or custom range)

Analysis: 4.4, 4.9 · Design: none (new in 0.9, user request 23 Nov 2026); the component is Kokonut UI's Receipt Printer · Mockup: none · Concepts: [period-overview](../concepts/period-overview.md), [notification](../concepts/notification.md), [category](../concepts/category.md), [budget](../concepts/budget.md), [design-system](../concepts/design-system.md) · Phases: [0.9-3c](../../plan/v0.9-notifications/03c-overview-receipt-data.md) (the data), [0.9-3d](../../plan/v0.9-notifications/03d-overview-receipt-screen.md) (this screen), [0.9-3e](../../plan/v0.9-notifications/03e-week-and-month-overview-notifications.md) (the notifications that open it)

## Content
What went out in a week, a month, a quarter, a year or a custom range of whole months (Overzicht's Maand, Kwartaal, Jaar and Eigen), as a **receipt that prints**: income, expenses per category with their subcategories and prices, saved, and what is left, with the comparison with the previous period and, for months only, each category against its budget. The data is `GET /api/reports/receipt?periode=week|maand|kwartaal|jaar|eigen&van=…[&tot=…]` (docs/api.md "Receipt"; `tot` for `eigen` only). It is the page behind the "Weekoverzicht" and "Maandoverzicht" notifications and is useful without them: Overzicht links to it for whatever period it has selected (the bill icon next to the account filter) and the page has its own Week, Maand, Kwartaal and Jaar picker.

The animated printer is **only on this page**. A push cannot render it (it stays generic text) and an e-mail cannot animate it (it carries a static receipt block with the same rows).

## Route and entry
`/overzicht/bon/:periode/:van`, `periode` `week`, `maand`, `kwartaal`, `jaar` or `eigen`, `van` a date in the period (the page rewrites it to the Monday, the 1st of the month, of the quarter or of the year; for `eigen` the 1st of its first month). `eigen` also needs `?tot=YYYY-MM-DD`, any date in its last month, which the page rewrites to that month's last day; a missing or impossible `tot` is an unknown page ("Deze pagina bestaat niet"), a `tot` before `van` is the server's message ("De einddatum ligt voor de begindatum."). `?scope=alles|persoonlijk|gedeeld` (the notifications always send `alles`; without it the app-wide scope). `?rekening=<id>` (a positive whole number, else ignored) is the one account the receipt is for, as in Overzicht's account filter: it goes to the API as `account` and the model's scope label says what it covers. The URL holds the period (a reload and Back work; changing the period replaces the entry).

**Entry points.** The notifications; and **the bill icon next to Overzicht's account filter**: a chip with a receipt glyph, 36 px like the filter, on every period (Maand, Kwartaal, Jaar and Eigen) and labelled with the selected period ("Bekijk de bon van oktober 2026", "…van K3 2026", "…van 2026", "…van mei – okt 2026"). It opens the receipt of the selected period (for Eigen the whole range) in the scope and for the account the viewer is in; see [overzicht](overzicht.md). The page's own picker reaches the rest.

## Layout (no board; derived from Overzicht and the component)
- **Header**: back chevron to Overzicht, title "Overzichtsbon" 26/600 (28/600 on desktop), `SegmentedControl` Week · Maand · Kwartaal · Jaar (four segments on one row at 375 px), previous and next arrows with labels ("Vorige week", "Volgende maand", "Vorig kwartaal", "Volgend jaar": the quarter and the year are neuter words; back to the first period of the history, forward to the running one), the scope switch of Overzicht. A **custom range** (`eigen`) has no segment selected and no arrows: the range ("Mei – oktober 2026") is shown where the arrows are, under a small note "Eigen periode" below the title, and a tap on a segment leaves the range for that kind.
- **The printer card**: the vendored Kokonut Receipt Printer (`web/src/ui/ReceiptPrinter.tsx`), card width `min(24rem, 100%)`, centred; housing label "Saldo", status "Aan het printen…" / "Trek naar beneden om af te scheuren"; the card grows with the paper (no clipping of the totals), the page scrolls. Prints when it scrolls into view (`autoPrint`), at once with reduced motion. Buttons: "Print de bon" / "Print nog een", and a visible "Scheur af" (the single-pointer alternative to dragging).
- **Under the card**: "Open in Overzicht" (everything but weeks) or "Bekijk de transacties" (weeks, a date-range drill-down), and the notes as a list (they are also printed on the paper). "Open in Overzicht" opens the custom range of the receipt's months, `/overzicht?periode=eigen&van=YYYY-MM&tot=YYYY-MM` (with `&rekening=<id>` for one account). That is all Overzicht's URL can pin: it has no URL state for a selected quarter or year, its Kwartaal view leaves out the running quarter and its Jaar view stops at the last complete month, so a quarter or a year shows there as its months, one column each.
- **States**: loading (a ghost paper), error (the usual card with "Opnieuw proberen"), empty period ("Geen transacties in deze week." / "…in deze maand." / "…in dit kwartaal." / "…in dit jaar." / "…in deze periode." with the arrows still working).

## The paper (top to bottom)
Monospace (Geist Mono 11 px, 15 px lines), one ink colour (`#27272A`) on off-white paper in both themes, a serrated bottom edge; amounts `1.234,56` from cents, minus U+2212, no currency symbol per line.
1. `SALDO`, the period ("Week 47 · 16–22 nov 2026" / "Oktober 2026" / "3e kwartaal 2026 · jul–sep" / "Jaar 2026" / "Mei – oktober 2026"), the scope ("Alle rekeningen samen · ter info"), a dotted rule, "bedragen in €".
2. **INKOMEN** total, one indented row per income category.
3. **UITGAVEN** total and its trend, then per category (largest first, zero rows hidden) a row with name, amount and trend ("+12 %", "−7 %", "= 0 %", "nieuw"); under it, indented, its **subcategories** with their amounts and, where something is booked on the category itself, the own-part row with the wording of the Overzicht breakdown ("<categorie> zelf"). On months only (not weeks, quarters, years or custom ranges) a second line under a category with a budget: "budget 550,00 · 117 %" or, without one, "gemiddeld 520,00 · 54 %" (the reference of [budget](../concepts/budget.md): Planning's budget, else the average of the last 6 months). "Niet gecategoriseerd" and the transfer rows ("Naar gedeelde rekening", "Naar eigen rekening") are rows like the others, in the same largest-first order as Overzicht's category list.
4. **GESPAARD** total, one row per saving category or savings account.
5. A dotted rule, **OVER** (income − expenses − saved) and, under it, the previous period's: "vorige week", "vorige maand", "vorig kwartaal", "vorig jaar" or, for a custom range, "vorige periode".
6. The notes: "Loopt nog, tot zo 22 nov: de bank geeft de transacties van vandaag pas morgen door.", "Niet volledig: Zichtrekening is niet bijgewerkt sinds ma 16 nov.", "Je geschiedenis begint op 19 nov."
7. A decorative barcode (`aria-hidden`) and the footer: "Tot volgende week" / "Tot volgende maand" (the two periods the notifications look ahead for), "Tot de volgende keer" for a quarter, a year and a custom range.

## Rules
- **Switching kind** (the segments) goes to the period of the new kind that **contains an anchor day**, never past the running period (it lands on the running period of the new kind instead). The anchor is the **last day of the current period** when the new kind is smaller (a month becomes the week that holds its last day, a quarter its last month or week, a year its last quarter, month or week; a running period counts as ending today) and the **first day of the current period** when the new kind is bigger (a week becomes the month, quarter or year of its Monday, a month its quarter or year). From a **custom range** every kind counts as smaller: the anchor is the range's last day. On 18 Nov 2026: 3e kwartaal 2026 to Maand is september; to Jaar is 2026; Jaar 2025 to Maand is december 2025; Jaar 2026 to Maand is november 2026 (the running month); the week of 29 Dec 2025 to Jaar is 2025 (its Monday); "Mei – oktober 2026" to Maand is oktober 2026 and to Week the week of 26 Oct.
- **The arrows** step 7 days, 1 month, 3 months or 1 year; forward never past the running period, back never before the history: the first period of the history is the last one back (it carries `partialFrom` and "Je geschiedenis begint op …" in its notes), and a period without any history is empty and the last one back too. A period that starts on the history's first day has no `partialFrom`, so its back arrow still leads one step, to an empty period.
- **The account** (`?rekening=`) stays in every link of the page (arrows, segments, "Open in Overzicht"), but **changing the scope on the page drops it** (it was picked in the old scope and may not belong to the new one); tapping the scope that is already chosen changes nothing.
- **The previous period** is last week, last month, the previous quarter, the previous calendar year, or for a custom range the range of as many whole months just before it.
- **The comparison runs against the previous full period even while the period is running** (a deviation from Overzicht, which gives a running period no trend): the receipt that a notification sends at 19:00 on the last day is still a running period. No comparison when the previous period is outside the history or the current one is a partial first period.
- **No verdicts** (risk 16): sums over several accounts are "ter info"; budgets are text, never coloured; the trend has no colour on the paper. In Alles a budget is the sum of each account's plan.
- **Pending rows never count** (as in every report); the snapshot of 19:00 therefore reads low for the last day or two, which is what the "Loopt nog" note says. The page itself is computed when opened.
- **Weeks are ISO weeks**, Monday to Sunday, Brussels dates; the label uses the ISO week-year.
- **Accessibility**: the paper is `role="document"` with an `aria-label` ("Bon: week 47, 16–22 nov"), real text in the DOM from the start (reading it does not wait for the printing); the status line is `aria-live="polite"`; dragging has a button alternative; "Print nog een" is reachable by keyboard after the tear; reduced motion shows the whole receipt at once; the arrows and the segmented control are labelled.
- **On a phone** a finger on the paper scrolls the page; the tear is started from the grab strip at the paper's lower edge ("Trek om af te scheuren") or with the button.
